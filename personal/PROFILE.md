# Personal QoL Profile

**Snapshot date:** 2026-09-15  
**Source:** interactive QoL audit using the repository catalog plus WHOQOL coverage review.  
**Status:** working profile; not a diagnosis, validated WHOQOL score, or treatment plan.

## Direction of life

Accepted summary:

> Build a stable and comfortable routine in which health is sufficiently cared for, work does not dominate life, time is protected for close relationships, friends and gaming/leisure, and money or technology are used to remove recurring friction rather than to maximize productivity.

The desired life is intentionally not a productivity-maximization project.

## Current overall picture

- Overall life satisfaction: reasonably satisfied, with several meaningful frictions.
- Direction/purpose: has a direction, but questions parts of it.
- Learning/growth: opportunities are available; time is the limiting factor.
- Financial situation: reasonably secure, with interest in increasing margin; willing to buy back time selectively.
- Access to health care: generally easy/acceptable, with some friction.
- Self-esteem / relationship with self: currently has moderate QoL impact and deserves further coverage-oriented assessment.
- Sexual-health domain: relevant but not currently a high personal priority.

## Time and weekly structure

- Work: approximately 8 hours/day, usually around 08:00-17:00 including 30-60 minutes of lunch; schedule has some flexibility. Approximate weekly work exposure is around 40 hours.
- Study, courses, technical projects or self-study outside work: approximately 5 hours/week.
- Commute: about 15 minutes each way when commuting.
- Desired weekly structure: 3 evenings free from meaningful work, mandatory study, or substantial domestic chores.
- When extra time appears, gaming is a common default and is also a genuinely valued leisure activity.
- The main perceived causes of time scarcity are low-value/automatic time use, recurring domestic work, and wanting to do many desirable things at once rather than clearly excessive paid work hours.

## High personal priorities

The explicit ranking among the four options compared on 2026-09-15 was:

1. Start strength training at home.
2. Recover time currently spent on cleaning/cooking.
3. Continue/complete work on chronic gastrointestinal symptoms.
4. Continue bruxism care, which is already improving.

Mental health is also an active and important QoL domain, but it was not included in that four-item ranking and therefore should not be assigned a numeric position without a later comparison.

## Physical activity

- Walking: about 30 minutes/day as two 15-minute bouts, roughly 4 days/week (~120 minutes/week).
- Satisfaction with walking: positive, with desire for a modest increase.
- Strength training: not currently performed regularly.
- Chosen modality: strength training at home.
- Space is available; some equipment is still needed.
- Preferred target style: establish frequency first rather than fix a session duration.
- Initial direction: start at 1 session/week and progress toward 2 sessions/week when sustainable.
- Sedentary exposure: many seated hours, with frequent getting up but occasional 1-2 hour uninterrupted periods.
- Low-back pain: rare at present and not a current active QoL priority.

## Sleep and environment

- Typical sleep duration: about 8-9 hours/night.
- Typical schedule: roughly 22:30-23:00 to 07:00-07:30.
- Sleep schedule appears reasonably regular.
- Restfulness varies; sometimes wakes wanting to stay in bed.
- Heat disrupts sleep some nights.
- Nighttime bathroom use usually follows waking for another reason rather than being the primary cause of waking.
- Hydration during the day is described as high/adequate.
- Night noise currently includes a newly adopted cat meowing at night.
- There are two cats: the resident cat is about 4 years old and calm; the newer cat was adopted about 3 weeks before this snapshot and is still adapting to the household routine.
- The desired approach is to reduce the new cat's nighttime disruption if possible without compromising its adjustment or welfare.

## Nutrition and substances

- Usual diet is described as mostly minimally processed foods, with frequent fruit/vegetable/legume intake.
- When tired or unwilling to cook, delivery is used while generally trying to preserve a minimally processed/nutritious pattern.
- Caffeine: about two small coffees in the morning plus one small coffee in the afternoon before 15:00.
- No caffeine-specific change is currently a personal target because no clear caffeine-linked sleep problem was established.
- Alcohol and nicotine status were not resolved in this audit and remain unknown rather than inferred.

## Attention and digital environment

- Most nonessential notifications are silenced; only important interruptions are allowed.
- Focus fragmentation comes from both self-initiated task switching and work that genuinely requires context switching.
- Reddit and ChatGPT are sometimes used more than desired.
- YouTube appears to consume the most time, often as background media during work, household tasks, gaming, meals, and before sleep.
- The personal goal is not to reduce total YouTube use by default. The target is to prevent background media from competing with activities that require sustained attention.
- During work/study, YouTube sometimes captures attention and interrupts reasoning.
- Generic "digital detox" is not a personal objective.

- Weekday time together can be as late as around 23:00, with more time on weekends.
- Desired next state: add approximately one intentional weekday evening together while preserving weekends.
- Friends: current contact feels somewhat lower than desired.

## Mental health and psychological well-being

Sensitive health context retained by explicit authorization:

- Diagnosed generalized anxiety disorder (TAG/GAD).
- Current medications reported: escitalopram 20 mg and pregabalin 75 mg.
- In therapy for about 2 years.
- Current stress/anxiety burden is between moderate and frequent/high and is considered an important QoL domain.
- Rumination/repetitive thinking is between occasional and frequent enough to consume attention/recovery.
- Loss of interest is usually better explained by fatigue or lack of time; persistent anhedonia was not established in this audit.
- Self-esteem currently has moderate impact and should be explored further using the expanded WHOQOL-informed methodology.
- Leisure is generally enjoyable, but sometimes mixed with thoughts of obligations or procrastination.

No inference is made here about adequacy of medication, diagnosis, or clinical treatment. Those are clinical matters outside the role of this profile.

## Gastrointestinal health

- Symptoms have been present for years.
- Reported pattern includes gas/bloating, alternating loose stools and constipation, and reflux/heartburn.
- Symptoms are frequent and can interfere with activities/leisure, although they are not described as causing major routine changes every day.
- Prior investigation has occurred partially.
- No blood in stool, unexplained weight loss, persistent severe pain, fever, or recurrent vomiting was reported in this audit.
- Chosen target: track symptom pattern/triggers and continue or complete professional investigation rather than assume a specific diagnosis or start arbitrary restrictive diets.
