# Free guided meditation app

A free guided meditation app is a concrete way to try QOL-061 when meditation or mindfulness is being used as a conditional tool rather than treated as a universal remedy.

Medito is one current example. Its first-party repository states that the app is free, has no ads, and does not require sign-up or payment. Product availability and features can change, so the canonical option remains generic rather than tied to one app.

## Evidence boundary

The linked QoL Item carries the evidence. This option does not imply that Medito, guided meditation apps as a class, or any specific meditation program independently improves stress, anxiety, attention, or quality of life.

See `docs/research/meditation-noise-apps-2026-09-13.md` for the product-source review and current example rationale.
