# Portable HEPA air purifier

A portable HEPA air cleaner is one filtration option when particulate exposure or another relevant indication justifies it. Device sizing, noise, filter replacement cost, and actual performance still require product-level evaluation.

See the [Implementation Selection Guide](../docs/selection-guides/IMP-017.md) for criteria to compare concrete air purifiers without treating a product recommendation as evidence for the parent QoL claim.
