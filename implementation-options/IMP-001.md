# Sleep mask

A sleep mask is a concrete way to apply QOL-010 when bedroom light cannot be reduced enough through the room itself.

The linked QoL Item carries the evidence. This option does not imply that a particular brand, material, shape, or price has independent evidence of improving sleep or quality of life.
