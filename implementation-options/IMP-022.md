# Free noise and ambient-sound generator app

A free noise and ambient-sound generator app is a concrete way to test sound masking under QOL-008 when intermittent environmental noise is a real sleep problem.

Metiq is one current example. Its first-party repository states that the Android app provides white, pink, brown, and grey noise, ambient sound mixes, a sleep timer, offline operation, and no account, tracking, or ads. Product availability and features can change, so the canonical option remains generic rather than tied to one app.

## Evidence boundary

The linked QoL Item carries the evidence. This option does not imply that continuous white, pink, or other generated noise improves sleep by default, and it does not treat Metiq's binaural-beat feature as evidence-backed.

See `docs/research/meditation-noise-apps-2026-09-13.md` for the product-source review and current example rationale.
