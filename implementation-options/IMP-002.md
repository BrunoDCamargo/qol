# Earplugs for nighttime noise

Earplugs are a concrete way to reduce nighttime noise exposure when source control or room changes are insufficient or impractical.

The linked QoL Item carries the evidence. No specific brand, material, attenuation rating, or price inherits evidence from the general intervention; fit, comfort, hearing needs, and safe use still matter.
