# Home cleaning service

A recurring or one-off cleaning service can apply QOL-022 by removing unwanted cleaning work and returning that time to the person buying the service.

## Evidence boundary

The linked QoL Item relies on evidence about time-saving purchases. That evidence does not show that every cleaning service improves well-being or that outsourcing cleaning is worthwhile at any price.
