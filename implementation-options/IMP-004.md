# Prepared meal service

Prepared meals, meal delivery, or comparable services can apply QOL-025 when they remove unwanted meal-planning, shopping, preparation, or cleanup time.

## Evidence boundary

The linked QoL Item relies on evidence about time-saving purchases. It does not establish that a particular meal service improves quality of life or provides adequate nutritional quality.
