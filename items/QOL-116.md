# Reduce hazardous occupational noise exposure and use hearing protection when indicated

Occupational noise is a preventable exposure when it reaches hazardous levels or recurs often enough to threaten hearing.

The preferred approach is exposure control at the source where feasible, with hearing protection used as part of a broader prevention program rather than as the only control.
