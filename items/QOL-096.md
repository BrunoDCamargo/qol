# Treat eczema or pruritus when it disrupts comfort or sleep

Chronic itch can be a sleep and quality-of-life problem rather than only a skin-appearance issue.

The intervention principle is to address symptomatic disease when present; treatment choice depends on cause, severity, and clinical context.
