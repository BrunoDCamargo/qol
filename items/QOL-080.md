# Measure actual weekly work hours when workload is uncertain

When workload feels high but the exposure is vague, measuring actual hours converts an impression into something that can inform a decision about work design and recovery.

The assessment tactic is inferred from population evidence on long working hours; the epidemiologic threshold should not be interpreted as a personalized safety cutoff.
