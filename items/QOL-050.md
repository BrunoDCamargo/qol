# Treat oral-health problems rather than normalizing pain or dysfunction

Persistent oral pain or dysfunction can directly interfere with eating, speaking, concentration, participation, and comfort.

The item is deliberately broad about treatment because the appropriate intervention depends on the underlying oral-health problem.
