# Treat persistent loss of interest, pleasure, or depression when present

Persistent depressive symptoms that impair life are legitimate treatment targets rather than states that must simply be endured.

Behavioral activation is one evidence-based option represented by the cited review; this item does not claim it is the only or best treatment for every person.
