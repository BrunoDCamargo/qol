# Replace saturated and trans fats with unsaturated fats or naturally fibre-rich carbohydrate sources

Fat quality matters as well as total dietary composition. Replacement is important: removing one source without considering what takes its place can miss the intended dietary change.

This item describes the substitution pattern supported by the guideline rather than endorsing one specific diet.
