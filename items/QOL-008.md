# Do not assume white or pink noise improves sleep; test conditionally

Continuous noise should not be treated as a universally beneficial sleep intervention. If used, its effect should be judged by the actual sleep problem and response.

This guardrail protects sleep quality from an appealing but insufficiently generalizable assumption about noise masking.
