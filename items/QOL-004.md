# Test caffeine timing when sleep may be affected

When caffeine may be contributing to sleep disruption, changing timing is a targeted experiment that preserves the option of caffeine use rather than assuming total abstinence is necessary.

The relevant QoL outcome is better sleep with the least unnecessary restriction, while recognizing that dose and individual sensitivity vary.
