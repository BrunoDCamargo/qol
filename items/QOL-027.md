# Pay for faster transport selectively when time savings justify it

A faster travel option can be treated as a time-saving purchase when it meaningfully shortens recurring or otherwise costly travel.

Transport itself was not tested in the cited experiment, so the benefit is an inference and depends on the actual time saved, cost, safety, and alternatives.
