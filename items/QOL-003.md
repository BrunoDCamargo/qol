# Use CBT-I for chronic insomnia when indicated

CBT-I is an evidence-based treatment option for persistent insomnia rather than a generic sleep-hygiene tip.

Improving clinically significant insomnia can reduce sleep-related symptoms and improve daily functioning and well-being.
