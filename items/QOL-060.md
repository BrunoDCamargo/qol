# Address persistent rumination when it consumes attention or recovery

Persistent repetitive thinking can become worth addressing when it repeatedly occupies attention or prevents recovery from work or other stressors.

The cited evidence is indirect for rumination as an isolated intervention target, so this item remains conditional and low-strength rather than presenting a generic anti-rumination technique as established treatment.
