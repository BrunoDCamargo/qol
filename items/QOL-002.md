# Evaluate possible sleep apnea when signs are present

When characteristic signs are present, assessment is more appropriate than treating poor sleep or daytime tiredness as a generic lifestyle problem.

Identifying a sleep-related breathing disorder can change the explanation and management of symptoms that affect sleep quality and daytime function.
