# Do not assume more social activities alone will resolve loneliness

Adding activities or contacts is not guaranteed to resolve loneliness, because loneliness is not reducible to the number of social opportunities available.

The cited trial directly supports this guardrail while leaving room for social interventions that better match the cause and context of loneliness.
