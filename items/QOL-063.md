# Prefer reliable recurring social contact over relying only on occasional large events

A dependable pattern of contact can be more useful for maintaining connection than treating rare large gatherings as the only social strategy.

This is an inference from evidence on social connection, not evidence that a specific frequency or event format is universally superior.
