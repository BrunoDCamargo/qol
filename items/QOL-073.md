# Evaluate symptomatic bruxism rather than normalizing it

Symptoms that cause pain, dental damage, or functional difficulty warrant assessment instead of being dismissed as harmless grinding or clenching.

This record is deliberately narrower than the legacy shorthand that also mentioned TMD because the migrated reference directly supports symptomatic bruxism, not temporomandibular disorders as a separate diagnosis.
