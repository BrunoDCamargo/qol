# Evaluate symptomatic bruxism or temporomandibular problems rather than normalizing them

Symptoms that cause pain, dental damage, or functional difficulty warrant assessment instead of being dismissed as harmless grinding or clenching.

The assessment should establish the actual problem before selecting a treatment.
