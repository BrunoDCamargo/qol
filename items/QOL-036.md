# Perform resistance training at least twice weekly when able

Muscle-strengthening activity is a distinct part of adult physical-activity guidance rather than a substitute for aerobic movement.

Maintaining strength and regular activity supports physical function and preventive health, with the exact exercises adapted to ability and context.
