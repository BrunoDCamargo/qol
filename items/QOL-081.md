# Improve psychological detachment from work when mental carryover impairs recovery

When work continues psychologically into non-work time, better detachment can improve the quality of recovery rather than merely increasing time away from work.

This item targets persistent mental carryover. It does not substitute for changing the work environment when structural conditions are the primary problem.
