# Do not assume prosocial acts are a universal treatment for loneliness

Prosocial behavior can be worthwhile for many reasons, but it should not be presented as a reliable standalone treatment for loneliness in everyone.

The cited randomized trial found heterogeneous effects, so the practical decision should depend on the person's actual social needs and response.
