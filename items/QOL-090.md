# Use microbreaks to manage fatigue during long cognitive work

Short breaks can be a practical way to manage fatigue during prolonged cognitive work without claiming a large productivity effect.

The evidence is stronger for fatigue and vigor than for performance, so success should be judged against the actual problem being targeted.
