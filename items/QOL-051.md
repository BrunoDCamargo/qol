# Evaluate hearing when functional difficulty is present

When hearing difficulty affects everyday function, evaluation can identify whether hearing loss or another problem is contributing and inform appropriate rehabilitation.

The QoL target is communication and participation rather than achieving a particular hearing-test number in isolation.
