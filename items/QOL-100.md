# Audit recurring commitments for whether they still justify their time, money, and recovery cost

Recurring commitments deserve periodic review because their costs repeat indefinitely while their original value can change.

The assessment asks whether an optional commitment would still be chosen today. Removal is a possible outcome when value has disappeared, not the definition of success.
