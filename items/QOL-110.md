# Treat menopause-associated symptoms when they impair well-being or function

Menopause-associated symptoms can affect several quality-of-life domains at once and can justify treatment when their burden is meaningful.

Management should be symptom-specific and individualized rather than assuming one hormonal or non-hormonal approach is appropriate for everyone.
