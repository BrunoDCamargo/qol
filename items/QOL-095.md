# Investigate chronic gastrointestinal symptoms that meaningfully affect quality of life

Recurring bowel symptoms deserve characterization when they materially affect daily life rather than automatic assignment to a popular diagnosis or dietary explanation.

Useful assessment includes the symptom pattern, duration, stool characteristics, triggers, medicines, diet, and clinically relevant warning features.
