# Reduce work-app intrusion into off-hours when feasible

When work tools repeatedly pull attention into work during time off, reducing their off-hours presence can create a clearer recovery boundary.

The relevant QoL target is stress and recovery during leisure time, subject to the practical demands and constraints of the person's role.
