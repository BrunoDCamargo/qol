# Correct meaningful refractive error and reassess vision when symptomatic

Visual symptoms or known refractive error are reasons to check whether correction is adequate rather than normalizing avoidable difficulty seeing.

The quality-of-life relevance is functional vision; other causes of symptoms still require appropriate assessment rather than assuming refraction is the explanation.
