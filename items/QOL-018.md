# Batch asynchronous communication when immediate response is unnecessary

Checking nonurgent communication in batches can reduce how often work is fragmented while preserving the communication channel.

Batching itself was not the tested intervention in the cited studies, so its QoL rationale is inferred from evidence about interruptions, strain, and multitasking.
