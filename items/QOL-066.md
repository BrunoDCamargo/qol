# Avoid assuming generic multivitamins will transform health or quality of life

A generic multivitamin should not be treated as a broad substitute for evidence-based prevention, nutrition, or treatment.

This guardrail does not rule out supplementation for a defined need; it limits unsupported claims of general transformation in otherwise healthy adults.
