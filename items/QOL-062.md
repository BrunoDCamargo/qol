# Maintain recurring contact with people one values

Recurring contact is a practical way to sustain social connection with relationships that matter.

The WHO evidence supports social connection as a quality-of-life determinant; the recommendation to maintain recurring contact is a practical inference rather than a directly tested schedule or dose.
