# Treat heavy menstrual bleeding according to its quality-of-life impact

Heavy menstrual bleeding is decision-relevant because of what it does to daily life, symptoms, and function, not only because of a measured volume.

The appropriate investigation and treatment depend on the underlying cause, preferences, and clinical context.
