# Create periods of monotasking

Deliberately working on one task at a time is a practical response to environments dominated by parallel work and interruptions.

The experiment establishes stress responses to multitasking and interruptions; prescribing monotasking periods is an inference intended to reduce that exposure and protect focused work.
