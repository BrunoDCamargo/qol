# Use hybrid work when role and circumstances support it

Hybrid work can change commuting burden and how work fits around the rest of life, but its feasibility depends strongly on the role and setting.

The cited trial supports a specific hybrid arrangement in one company, so the QoL implication should not be generalized to every job or worker.
