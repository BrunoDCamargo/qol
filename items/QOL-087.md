# Increase financial safety margin when financial insecurity is a meaningful stressor

A financial buffer can be relevant when uncertainty itself repeatedly consumes attention or produces stress.

The intervention is inferred from evidence about financial hardship. The appropriate buffer depends on income, obligations, risk, and competing needs rather than a universal number.
