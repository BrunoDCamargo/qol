# Increase useful daytime light exposure

Daytime light exposure is a practical way to work with the environmental light-dark signal that helps regulate circadian timing.

The source establishes the role of light in the sleep-wake cycle; the recommendation to deliberately increase useful daytime exposure is a practical inference rather than a directly tested intervention in that source.
