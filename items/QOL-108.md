# Use supervised pelvic floor muscle training for stress or mixed urinary incontinence

Pelvic floor muscle training is a specific therapeutic intervention for stress or mixed urinary incontinence rather than a generic pelvic-health exercise recommendation.

Supervision and matching the treatment to the symptom pattern are part of the evidence-based approach.
