# Keep the phone physically out of reach during focus activities

Physical separation adds friction to habitual checking without requiring permanent removal of the device or its services.

The cited studies tested connectivity and notifications rather than phone placement, so the specific placement strategy is an inference aimed at reducing interruptions during valued focus activities.
