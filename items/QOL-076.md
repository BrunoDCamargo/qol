# Treat recurrent low-back pain rather than normalizing it

Persistent or recurrent low-back pain that affects daily life is a problem to assess and manage, not an inevitable cost of work or aging.

The appropriate management depends on the presentation; the guideline supports multiple options rather than a single universal treatment.
