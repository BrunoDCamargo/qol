# Schedule meaningful or rewarding activities when low activation is part of depression or avoidance

Planned activity can be a treatment component when withdrawal and low activation are maintaining a depressive pattern.

The item is narrower than generic advice to stay busy: activity selection and follow-through should fit the behavioral mechanism and clinical context.
