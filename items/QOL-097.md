# Review medications when symptoms plausibly track medication initiation or dose changes

Medication timing belongs in the differential when a symptom starts or changes near a medication change.

The purpose is review with the prescriber, pharmacist, or other appropriate clinician, not unilateral discontinuation based on timing alone.
