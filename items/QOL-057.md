# Trial lower alcohol exposure when alcohol may affect sleep, mood, or health

Reducing alcohol exposure is a reversible way to test whether it is contributing to a concern while also reducing a recognized health exposure.

Because the cited guidance is broader than sleep or mood outcomes, those symptom-specific applications are treated as an inference rather than a guaranteed effect.
