# Repair or replace recurring physical irritants when doing so reliably removes repeated friction

Small irritations can become meaningful when they repeat every day. A repair or replacement can be worthwhile when it predictably removes that repeated cost.

Require an observed recurring problem before applying the principle; the goal is friction reduction, not consumption for its own sake.
