# Consider living closer to recurring destinations when commute dominates life

Where someone lives can determine a large recurring time cost, so relocation can be evaluated as one way to reduce unwanted travel rather than only as a housing choice.

The evidence supports the value of time-saving and one alternative to commuting, not relocation itself. Moving is therefore an inferred and highly conditional option with financial, social, and environmental trade-offs.
