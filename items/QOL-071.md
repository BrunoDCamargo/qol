# Treat persistent rhinitis or congestion when it affects sleep or daytime function

When nasal symptoms repeatedly disturb sleep or daily function, addressing the underlying cause can be more useful than accepting the impairment as normal.

Treatment choice depends on the cause; the cited trial should not be generalized to all forms of congestion.
