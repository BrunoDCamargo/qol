# Reduce bright light late at night when circadian delay is a concern

Reducing unnecessary bright evening light is relevant when late light exposure may be pushing sleep timing later.

The QoL relevance is tied to sleep timing and continuity, not to treating all evening screen or light exposure as inherently harmful.
