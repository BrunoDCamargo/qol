# Treat recurrent neck pain rather than normalizing it

Recurrent neck pain that affects work or daily function is a problem to assess and manage rather than an inevitable cost of desk work.

The cited trial shows that intervention can improve pain-related and productivity outcomes in symptomatic office workers, while the appropriate treatment still depends on the individual presentation.
