# Treat meditation or mindfulness as a conditional tool rather than a universal remedy

Mindfulness can be tried when it fits the person and the problem, but it should not be assumed to work for everyone or for every mental-health goal.

The cited evidence supports caution about universal claims and does not justify replacing indicated clinical treatment with a generic mindfulness practice.
