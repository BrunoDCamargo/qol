# Treat clinically relevant nocturnal reflux

Reflux that repeatedly disturbs sleep or function is worth managing as a clinical problem rather than accepting the nighttime disruption.

Management should follow the diagnosis and symptom pattern; evidence for improvement in broader sleep outcomes is not uniform.
