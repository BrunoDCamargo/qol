# Prefer whole grains, vegetables, fruits, and pulses as primary carbohydrate sources

Diet quality depends on carbohydrate source as well as total intake. Building routine meals around whole grains, vegetables, fruits, and pulses supports a dietary pattern aligned with current WHO guidance.

This is a food-pattern principle rather than a requirement to maximize carbohydrate intake or use any single food.
