# Move discretionary social-media use to less interruptive devices

Moving optional social-media access away from the always-carried phone can preserve access while changing how immediately available it is.

The cited trial tested mobile-internet blocking rather than device substitution, so this is an inferred implementation aimed at attention and well-being rather than a directly proven social-media treatment.
