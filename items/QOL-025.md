# Use prepared meals or meal services when they meaningfully save unwanted time

Prepared meals, meal delivery, or comparable services can reduce unwanted time spent acquiring and preparing food.

The cited experiment supports the broader time-saving purchase principle. It does not establish that every meal service improves well-being or nutritional quality.
