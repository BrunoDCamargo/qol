# Maintain adequate dietary fibre intake from foods

Adequate fibre intake supports a broader dietary pattern built from fibre-rich foods rather than treating fibre as an isolated optimization target.

The adult guideline provides a population target; individual dietary needs and tolerance still depend on context.
