# Combine ergonomics with movement or exercise rather than relying only on gadgets

Ergonomic equipment can be part of a response to work-related symptoms, but the intervention should not be reduced to buying hardware while ignoring movement or exercise.

The QoL target is improved comfort and work function through a combined approach rather than an unsupported assumption that a particular gadget alone solves the problem.
