# Evaluate recurrent nocturia when it disrupts sleep

The first step is to distinguish waking because of urinary need from choosing to urinate after waking for another reason.

Quantifying episodes and context can guide whether the relevant next step is urinary, sleep, medication, fluid-timing, or other assessment.
