# Consider cataract surgery when cataract-related vision loss interferes with everyday activities

Cataract treatment becomes quality-of-life relevant when visual loss limits tasks the person values or needs to perform.

The decision is driven by functional impact and clinical context rather than the mere presence of a cataract.
