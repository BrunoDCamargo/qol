# Maintain sufficient sleep duration

Regularly obtaining sufficient sleep is a basic sleep-health intervention rather than a performance optimization trick.

Insufficient sleep can affect health and daytime functioning, so sleep duration is directly relevant to quality of life while still allowing for individual variation.
