# Address digital eye strain when screen work causes symptoms

Recurring symptoms during screen work justify adjusting the work pattern and considering other causes rather than simply tolerating discomfort.

Breaks are one supported tool, but persistent or atypical symptoms can require broader visual or clinical assessment.
