# Trial reduced or blocked mobile internet on smartphones

Temporarily reducing mobile internet access is a reversible way to test whether always-available connectivity is affecting attention or well-being.

The intervention is relevant to quality of life because the trial measured sustained attention, mental health, and subjective well-being rather than screen time alone.
