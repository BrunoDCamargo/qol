# Reduce reliance on ultraprocessed foods

Reducing reliance on ultra-processed foods is a dietary-pattern intervention rather than a rule that every processed food is harmful.

The relevant QoL and preventive-health rationale concerns overall intake patterns, food quality, and weight-related outcomes, not a binary label applied to individual foods.
