# Avoid treating consumer CO2 sensing as a guaranteed cognitive-performance hack

A consumer CO2 reading can be useful context for ventilation, but it should not be converted automatically into a claim about a person's cognitive performance.

This guardrail keeps indoor-air decisions tied to what a sensor measures and what the evidence actually demonstrates.
