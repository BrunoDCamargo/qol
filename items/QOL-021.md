# Spend money to buy back unwanted time when worthwhile

Some discretionary spending can be evaluated by the unwanted time it removes rather than only by the object or service purchased.

The QoL trade-off is conditional on affordability and on the purchase actually reducing time pressure or freeing time for something more valued.
