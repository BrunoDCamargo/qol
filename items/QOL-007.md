# Reduce meaningful nighttime environmental noise

Nighttime noise can disrupt sleep. Reduce the source, change the environment, or use a suitable barrier when the exposure is meaningful.

Earplugs are one possible implementation. Their specific evidence base is narrower than the general evidence for reducing harmful nighttime noise.
