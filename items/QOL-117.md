# Reduce sustained work weeks of 55 hours or more when work design can be changed

When very long work weeks are persistent rather than exceptional, reducing sustained exposure is a reasonable work-design target.

The item is inferred from population risk evidence. It does not turn 55 hours into a personalized safety cutoff or imply that one exact schedule change has a guaranteed effect.
