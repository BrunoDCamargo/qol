# Include sexual health in quality-of-life assessment when relevant

Sexual health can materially affect well-being and should not be excluded from a quality-of-life assessment when it is relevant to the person.

This item is about making the domain assessable; it does not prescribe a specific intervention or assume sexual concerns are present for everyone.
