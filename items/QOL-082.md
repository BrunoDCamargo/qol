# Build deliberate recovery skills after work when recovery is persistently poor

Recovery can be treated as a skill when a person has time away from work but still fails to recover well enough for sleep, mood, function, or the next work period.

The evidence is promising but small, so this remains a conditional option rather than a universal prescription.
