# Use evidence-based psychological treatment for clinically significant anxiety

Clinically significant anxiety is a treatment target rather than something that must simply be tolerated or managed through generic wellness tactics.

Treatment choice should match the actual presentation, severity, preferences, and access to care rather than assuming one protocol fits every anxiety problem.
