# Treat persistent relationship distress when it is a major quality-of-life driver

When relationship distress is a major source of impairment or reduced well-being, it can be an appropriate target for structured help rather than being treated as outside health and quality of life.

The cited evidence supports couple therapy for relevant distress while making clear that response varies and context matters.
