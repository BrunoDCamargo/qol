# Use hearing rehabilitation when meaningful hearing loss is identified

Hearing rehabilitation can improve access to communication and participation when hearing loss creates real functional barriers.

The intervention follows identification of meaningful hearing loss; it is not a substitute for evaluation of unexplained or changing hearing symptoms.
