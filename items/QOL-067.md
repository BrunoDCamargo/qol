# Avoid indiscriminate vitamin-D screening in asymptomatic adults without indication

Routine screening should not be treated as automatically beneficial when there is no symptom, risk factor, or other clinical indication.

The evidence boundary is population screening in asymptomatic adults; this does not argue against testing when an individual indication exists.
