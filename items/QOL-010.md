# Use blackout or a sleep mask when bedroom light is disruptive

A sleep mask or blackout treatment can reduce unwanted bedroom light when darkness cannot be achieved through the room itself.

The practical recommendation extrapolates beyond a trial that tested an eye mask together with earplugs in a specific population. It should not be read as evidence for any particular product.
