# R&D Career Radar: remote-Brazil expansion 2

## Summary

This pass looked for additional organizations where a professional living in Curitiba can build a sustained R&D career remotely from Brazil. Three organizations pass the current inclusion rule: BeOne Medicines, Adaption, and Roboflow.

Instituto Recôncavo de Tecnologia remains under investigation because its institutional site strongly establishes PD&I and a recurring careers channel, but current remote-Brazil evidence was not verified from a sufficiently strong first-party source in this pass.

## Verified additions

### BeOne Medicines

BeOne has a large internal oncology R&D organization rather than operating only as a clinical-services provider. Its institutional R&D page states that the company has almost 5,000 scientists, clinicians and innovators across biology, chemistry, pharmacology, translational medicine and clinical science, and that it manages its extensive clinical research program in-house. [BeOne — Research & Development](https://beonemedicines.com/science/research-and-development/)

Its current pipeline includes more than 40 clinical and commercial assets and more than 190 clinical trials globally. [BeOne — Pipeline](https://beonemedicines.com/science/pipeline/)

BeOne maintains a recurring institutional careers portal. [BeOne — Careers](https://beonemedicines.com/careers/)

Current employer-controlled Workday listings show multiple full-time positions explicitly located in `Brazil Remote`, including Clinical Research Associate I, Associate Director Safety Scientist and global safety-management roles. [Clinical Research Associate I](https://beigene.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/beigene/job/brazil-remote/clinical-research-associate-i_r36135) [Associate Director, Safety Scientist](https://beigene.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/zh-CN/BeiGene/job/Associate-Director--Safety-Scientist_R34457) [Senior Specialist, Global ICSR Management](https://beigene.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/en-US/BeiGene/job/Senior-Specialist--Global-ICSR-Management_R36248)

**Decision:** promote as a primary remote-from-Brazil R&D career target. Although some current Brazil roles are clinical-development and safety functions, they are part of the company's own vertically integrated R&D organization and drug pipeline, not external CRO assignments.

### Adaption

Adaption is an AI research company focused on adaptive intelligence. Its research site presents current original work on frontier AI capabilities, adaptive data and real-world AI use, and says its team collectively has more than 150 publications across leading AI conferences. [Adaption — Research](https://adaptionlabs.ai/research)

The institutional careers page currently lists multiple Modelling positions open to Brazil, including full-time remote `Research Scientist / Research Engineer` and `Senior Research Scientist`. [Adaption — Careers](https://adaptionlabs.ai/careers)

The Research Scientist / Research Engineer role explicitly lists Brazil among eligible locations, is full-time and remote, and describes frontier research on model efficiency, real-time learning, alignment and algorithmic optimization. [Research Scientist / Research Engineer](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/adaption/5635dd7e-a0ca-414d-b738-71500502dba9)

**Decision:** promote as a primary remote-from-Brazil R&D career target.

### Roboflow

Roboflow develops computer-vision and machine-learning technology and maintains a formal internal research team. Its current Research Scientist role states that the research team originates novel ML research, develops theoretical approaches and algorithms, publishes at top conferences and works with engineering to translate research into production systems. [Roboflow — Research Scientist](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/roboflow/40c3389e-c7ea-4054-8c90-05b1beb38bff)

The company also maintains a dedicated research site with work presented at ICLR, NeurIPS, ICCV and CVPR and an active research program. [Roboflow Research](https://research.roboflow.com/)

Roboflow's institutional careers page describes the company as distributed and remote-capable. [Roboflow — Careers](https://roboflow.com/careers)

The current Research Scientist posting says the team is distributed globally, explicitly names a Brazil hub and allows employees to work from home, a hub or coworking spaces. The company's description of its distributed-work model also says the team spans Brazil and other countries. [Research Scientist](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/roboflow/40c3389e-c7ea-4054-8c90-05b1beb38bff) [Distributed Work](https://blog.roboflow.com/how-we-work-together-at-roboflow/)

**Decision:** promote as a primary remote-from-Brazil R&D career target.

## Keep under investigation

### Instituto Recôncavo de Tecnologia

The Instituto Recôncavo de Tecnologia clearly meets the R&D relevance gate. Its institutional site defines it as a private nonprofit ICT created to perform PD&I in information and communication technology and reports more than 400 projects across AI, computer vision, Industry 4.0, IoT, electronics and other areas. It also maintains a recurring `Trabalhe Conosco` section and a general CV channel. [Instituto Recôncavo](https://www.reconcavo.org.br/)

A current remote software role was found during discovery, but the remote-work evidence was not available on the institutional site in this pass. Under the Career Radar's primary-source rule, that is not enough to promote the organization as remote-Brazil yet.

**Decision:** keep under investigation pending current first-party confirmation that recurring employment can be performed remotely from Brazil.

## Notes on candidates not promoted

- Sidia and INDT have very strong first-party PD&I evidence, but current first-party material found in this pass points mainly to location-specific work rather than recurring remote-Brazil employment.
- dtLabs has a compelling current remote P&D role in AI and robotics, but the discoverable evidence in this pass came from LinkedIn rather than a stable institutional careers channel, so it was not promoted.
