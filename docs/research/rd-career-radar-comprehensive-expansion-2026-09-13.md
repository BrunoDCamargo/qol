# R&D Career Radar: comprehensive sector-agnostic expansion

Date: 2026-09-13

## Scope and method

This pass follows the durable scope in `career-radar/FOCUS.md`:

- discovery is sector-agnostic;
- Curitiba/RMC and sustained remote-Brazil work have equal priority;
- promotion requires all three gates: meaningful recurring R&D/research/development activity, practical viability while living in Curitiba, and a recurring institutional career path;
- sector labels are metadata applied after discovery, not search filters or quotas;
- only first-party organization pages and employer-controlled recruiting systems are used as promotion evidence.

The search deliberately crossed unrelated organization types and domains, including industrial manufacturing, animal nutrition, telecommunications, plant breeding, pharmaceuticals, power systems, diagnostics, veterinary health, furniture/product engineering, coatings, higher education, enterprise software, advertising technology, blockchain intelligence, fintech and AI research.

## Search-quality observations

The promoted set is intentionally heterogeneous, but no sector received a quota. The point of crossing many unrelated industries is to reduce blind spots created by the existing catalog. Future comprehensive passes should continue changing discovery vocabulary and organization types instead of repeatedly mining whichever sectors already produce easy results.

The strict gate remains more important than maximizing entry count: strong R&D without Curitiba viability, strong careers without R&D, and one-off or contractor-only opportunities stay outside the primary target list until all conditions are satisfied.
