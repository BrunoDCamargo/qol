# Which new R&D career targets should be added to the Curitiba Career Radar on 2026-09-14?

## Summary

A new local/RMC research pass identified seven organizations that meet the current Career Radar inclusion rule: meaningful R&D or engineering-development activity, practical viability while living in Curitiba, and a recurring employment channel.

Promoted in this batch:

- Grupo Lumicenter Lighting
- Siderquímica
- Microcapsules Tech
- BrasilSat Harald
- Leclair Cosméticos
- Isogama
- GRECA Asfaltos

The pass also surfaced Apolo Lab, Latinex Brands, Vidal Life, Charlotte Pães, Rede HDS, Trombini and TOTVS Curitiba. They were not promoted in this batch because the available evidence did not yet establish the full combination of durable local R&D activity and a recurring career path strongly enough under the current focus rules.

## Findings

### Grupo Lumicenter Lighting

Verified fact: Lumicenter's own company history documents a dedicated Pesquisa e Desenvolvimento team, electronics development, photometric laboratories and continuous development and improvement of LED lighting products. Its matrix and factory are in São José dos Pinhais.

Verified fact: Lumicenter maintains a recurring recruitment portal. A 2026 posting for `Estágio P&D Eletrônicos - Desenvolvimento de Produtos` describes product development, testing, implementation, technical specification analysis and component homologation in São José dos Pinhais.

Disposition: promote as a local industrial R&D target.

### Siderquímica

Verified fact: Siderquímica's official site identifies a Pesquisa e Desenvolvimento sector and describes development of chemical technologies for the textile, pulp, paper and wood-board industries. Its São José dos Pinhais operation is listed on the same site.

Verified fact: the company maintains an official `Trabalhe Conosco` page and recruiting email.

Disposition: promote as a local chemicals/materials R&D target.

### Microcapsules Tech

Verified fact: Microcapsules Tech describes technology platforms for micro- and nanoencapsulation of microorganisms, metabolites, RNAi, botanical extracts and related biological inputs from its Curitiba site at the UTFPR technology park.

Verified fact: the company has repeatedly recruited for P&D work. A current 2026 posting describes laboratory R&D in microencapsulation, experimentation and technology development, while earlier company postings also advertised P&D internships and scale-up work.

Disposition: promote as a local biotech/agtech applied-R&D target. The organization is small, so vacancy frequency may be lower than at larger employers.

### BrasilSat Harald

Verified fact: BrasilSat states that it maintains a Pesquisa e Desenvolvimento group with laboratories, test fields, simulation tools and continued technology investment. Its activities span telecommunications infrastructure, energy and agribusiness engineering.

Verified fact: BrasilSat maintains a recurring recruitment portal with many active processes in Curitiba.

Disposition: promote as a local engineering and industrial R&D target.

### Leclair Cosméticos

Verified fact: Leclair has directly recruited for formulation P&D in São José dos Pinhais. The role covered formulation development and optimization, pilot tests, scale-up, stability, technical documentation, risk analysis and technology transfer.

Verified fact: the company maintains an official `Trabalhe Conosco` channel with a dedicated recruiting email.

Disposition: promote as a local cosmetics/formulation R&D target.

### Isogama

Verified fact: Isogama's official innovation page states that the company has a Research and Development structure equipped for new-product creation, analyses, customization and chemical-product development. Its Paraná unit is in São José dos Pinhais.

Verified fact: Isogama maintains an official `Trabalhe Conosco` page with a standing curriculum form for future opportunities.

Disposition: promote as a local chemicals/materials R&D target.

### GRECA Asfaltos

Verified fact: GRECA's official site describes a Centro de Pesquisa, Desenvolvimento & Inovação that develops asphalt technologies and performs laboratory testing and applied technical research. The group cites its asphalt-rubber technology as an example of work developed through this structure.

Verified fact: GRECA's matrix is in Araucária, which is part of the Curitiba metropolitan region, and it maintains a recurring recruitment portal with positions in Araucária and Colombo.

Disposition: promote as a local materials/civil-engineering R&D target.

### Candidates not promoted in this pass

Apolo Lab has strong evidence of genuine P&D in energy, biogas, biomethane and industrial processes, including ANEEL R&D projects. However, a stable employment or fellowship channel was not located during this pass.

Latinex Brands has a current R&D role in São José dos Pinhais, but the available evidence was dominated by a single vacancy and no sufficiently stable first-party career channel was confirmed in this pass.

Vidal Life has a current P&D role and an official site that emphasizes product development and innovation, but a durable first-party recruitment channel was not confirmed.

Charlotte Pães has a stable `Trabalhe Conosco` contact and a current P&D role, but the available company-level evidence is still closer to product development and process innovation than to the stronger R&D standard used for promotion here.

Rede HDS has a current assistant-level Pesquisa e Desenvolvimento vacancy in Curitiba, but the described work is primarily product registration, drawing checks and support for new item development. That evidence is not yet strong enough to establish a sustained R&D career environment.

Trombini maintains Curitiba operations and a stable careers portal, but the company's own site places its explicit product-development unit in Farroupilha, RS rather than Curitiba. It was not promoted as a Curitiba-based R&D target.

TOTVS Curitiba is a local franchise unit of a company with substantial corporate R&D investment, but the evidence reviewed does not establish that the Curitiba franchise itself provides a local R&D career environment.

## Gaps and caveats

The Career Radar validates structure offline but does not prove link freshness. Current hiring and location evidence should continue to be reviewed during later research passes.

Microcapsules Tech is a small company. Its repeated P&D hiring supports inclusion, but career-path durability is inherently less certain than at larger organizations.

Leclair's R&D evidence is employer-authored but hosted on LinkedIn rather than the company's own domain. The official company site independently confirms the recurring recruitment channel.

This pass focused on Curitiba and the RMC. It does not reduce the priority of remote-Brazil targets; it only reflects the strongest additions found in this search cycle.
