# R&D Career Radar: comprehensive expansion, batch 4

Date: 2026-09-13

## Scope and method

This pass follows `career-radar/FOCUS.md`: discovery remains sector-agnostic, Curitiba/RMC and sustained remote-Brazil work have equal priority, and promotion requires meaningful recurring research, R&D, engineering-development, product-development or scientific activity, practical viability while living in Curitiba, and a recurring institutional career path.

Only first-party organization pages and employer-controlled recruiting systems are used as promotion evidence. The machine-readable entries from this batch are recorded in `career-radar/organizations-expansion-2026-09-13-4.yaml`.

## Search-quality observations

This pass crossed molecular biology/diagnostics, animal health, electrical materials, heavy industrial equipment, forestry/commodity research and cybersecurity. That diversity emerged from broad discovery rather than a sector quota.

The strict gate remains more important than entry count. Strong R&D without a recurring career route, recurring careers without sufficient R&D evidence, or stale/contract-only remote evidence does not enter the primary target tier.
