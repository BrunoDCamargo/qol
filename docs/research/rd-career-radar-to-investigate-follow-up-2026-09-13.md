# R&D Career Radar: `To investigate` follow-up

## Summary

This pass rechecked every candidate currently listed under `career-radar/README.md` -> `To investigate` against the catalog inclusion rule:

1. meaningful R&D / research relevance supported by primary evidence;
2. practical viability for someone living in Curitiba, either locally or through remote-Brazil work;
3. a recurring institutional opportunity channel.

Nine candidates now meet the rule and should be promoted to `organizations.yaml`: Neodent, BEAUTYCOLOR Company, Perkons, ATEEI, Instituto Atlântico, Kunumi, Vector Trading, EDETEK, and Turing.

Five candidates should remain under investigation: CPQD, Human Robotics, Microcapsules Tech, Pumatronix, and De Cosméticos.

### Keep under investigation

The visible current opportunity is hybrid in Campinas, and recent research openings indexed by the portal are also hybrid in Campinas. #### De Cosméticos

- De Cosméticos operates in São José dos Pinhais and states that its pharmacists and chemists have 19 years of experience in research and development of cosmetic formulations. [De Cosméticos](https://decosmeticos.com.br/)
- No stable recurring institutional careers/jobs page was verified. Keep pending on the opportunity-channel criterion.

## Recommendation

Promote the nine accepted candidates to `career-radar/organizations.yaml`, remove them from `README.md` -> `To investigate`, and retain the five unresolved candidates with the narrower blockers documented above.

The strict rule should remain unchanged. Evidence that an organization develops products is not automatically equivalent to an R&D signal, and historical remote-work evidence should not be treated as current geographic viability when the current job board points elsewhere.
