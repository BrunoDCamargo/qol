# R&D Career Radar HiringCafe double check

## Summary

HiringCafe was used only as a discovery surface. Acceptance decisions were re-checked against first-party sources and the Career Radar inclusion rule: meaningful R&D or research activity, viability for someone living in Curitiba, and a recurring institutional opportunity channel.

Six organizations passed the full evidence chain in this pass and were added to the accepted catalog:

- SEGULA Technologies Brasil;
- Parexel;
- Syneos Health;
- MMS Holdings;
- IQVIA;
- Thermo Fisher Scientific / PPD.

Precision for Medicine, Kunumi and Vector Trading remain in `To investigate` because one part of the geographic viability evidence was not strong enough in current first-party material.

## Accepted organizations

### SEGULA Technologies Brasil

- **R&D / development fact:** SEGULA's 2026 Brazilian recruiting announcement describes a Global Centre of Excellence for Agricultural Vehicles in Curitiba dedicated to the design, development and industrialization of next-generation agricultural machinery. The same announcement highlights product development, embedded electronics, automation and advanced technology roles. [SEGULA Technologies Brasil](https://brasil.segulatechnologies.com/pt/news/o-grupo-de-engenharia-segula-technologies-brasil-vai-recrutar-mais-de-600-profissionais-em-2026/)
- **Geographic fact:** the engineering center is in Curitiba, so onsite work is viable from Curitiba. [SEGULA Technologies Brasil](https://brasil.segulatechnologies.com/pt/news/o-grupo-de-engenharia-segula-technologies-brasil-vai-recrutar-mais-de-600-profissionais-em-2026/)
- **Opportunity channel:** SEGULA maintains a recurring careers portal. [SEGULA Careers](https://careers.segulatechnologies.com/pt_BR/annonces)
- **Decision:** Accept as Curitiba/RMC.

### Parexel

- **Research fact:** Parexel's careers material is organized around clinical research and drug-development functions, including clinical research associates and related development roles. [Parexel Clinical Research Associates](https://jobs.parexel.com/en/clinical-research-associates-jobs)
- **Geographic fact:** Parexel's official jobs portal publishes positions explicitly marked `Brazil, Remote`, including clinical-research roles. [Parexel Jobs](https://jobs.parexel.com/en/search_jobs)
- **Opportunity channel:** the same jobs portal is recurring and supports Brazil as a location. [Parexel Jobs](https://jobs.parexel.com/en/search_jobs)
- **Decision:** Accept as remote Brazil.

### Syneos Health

- **Research fact:** Syneos Health describes Clinical Development as work spanning Phase I-IV research, monitoring and clinical-trial execution. [Syneos Health Clinical Development](https://www.syneoshealth.com/clinical-corporate-careers/pages/clinical-development)
- **Geographic fact:** its official careers portal publishes Brazil roles explicitly identified as remote/home-based, including CRA positions. [Syneos Health Careers](https://www.syneoshealth.com/clinical-corporate-careers/search/jobs)
- **Opportunity channel:** the official clinical and corporate careers search is recurring. [Syneos Health Careers](https://www.syneoshealth.com/clinical-corporate-careers/search/jobs)
- **Decision:** Accept as remote Brazil.

### MMS Holdings

- **Research fact:** MMS identifies itself as a global, data-focused CRO supporting drug development, biostatistics, data science, medical writing and regulatory work. [MMS About](https://mmsholdings.com/about/)
- **Geographic fact:** its official careers page lists remote Brazil positions, including clinical data and statistical-programming roles. [MMS Careers](https://mmsholdings.com/careers/)
- **Opportunity channel:** the careers page is a recurring institutional job portal. [MMS Careers](https://mmsholdings.com/careers/)
- **Decision:** Accept as remote Brazil.

### IQVIA

- **Research fact:** IQVIA maintains Clinical Research as a formal career area covering clinical science, clinical operations, monitoring and related development activities. [IQVIA Clinical Research](https://jobs.iqvia.com/en/clinical-research)
- **Geographic fact:** current official postings include home-based Brazil positions and state that some roles can be performed from anywhere in Brazil. [IQVIA Jobs](https://jobs.iqvia.com/en/jobs)
- **Opportunity channel:** IQVIA maintains a recurring global jobs portal with Brazil filters and positions. [IQVIA Jobs](https://jobs.iqvia.com/en/jobs)
- **Decision:** Accept as remote Brazil.

### Thermo Fisher Scientific / PPD

- **Research fact:** Thermo Fisher maintains dedicated Research & Development and Clinical Research career areas. The PPD clinical-research business supports clinical trials and drug development, while Thermo Fisher reports thousands of scientists and engineers in R&D. [Thermo Fisher R&D](https://jobs.thermofisher.com/global/en/research-and-development) [Thermo Fisher Clinical Research](https://jobs.thermofisher.com/global/en/clinical-research)
- **Geographic fact:** the official careers site maintains a Brazil jobs page and has published remote São Paulo clinical-research roles, establishing a recurring remote-work path for Brazil-based staff. [Thermo Fisher Brazil Jobs](https://jobs.thermofisher.com/global/en/brazil-jobs)
- **Opportunity channel:** the Brazil jobs portal is recurring. [Thermo Fisher Brazil Jobs](https://jobs.thermofisher.com/global/en/brazil-jobs)
- **Decision:** Accept as remote Brazil.

## Held for investigation

- **Gap:** current first-party material reviewed in this pass did not establish remote-Brazil eligibility with the same confidence as the accepted CROs.

### Kunumi

- **Verified:** Kunumi explicitly presents AI research and advanced research work and maintains a careers page. [Kunumi Careers](https://www.kunumi.com/careers)
- **Gap:** current first-party evidence did not explicitly establish Curitiba or remote-Brazil work eligibility.
- **Decision:** Keep in `To investigate`.

### Vector Trading

- **Verified:** Vector Trading describes quantitative research and systematic, scientific model development and maintains an active careers portal. [Vector Trading Open Roles](https://www.vectortrading.com/open-roles)
- **Gap:** current first-party evidence did not explicitly establish remote-Brazil eligibility for its research roles.
- **Decision:** Keep in `To investigate`.

## Discovery-only names

The HiringCafe screenshots also surfaced organizations and roles whose use of `research` did not by itself establish the Career Radar's R&D criterion. These were not promoted in this pass. Examples include survey/research-platform work, AI-training marketplaces, editing services and general market-research roles.

HiringCafe remains useful as a discovery mechanism, but it is not used as evidence for acceptance into the structured catalog.
