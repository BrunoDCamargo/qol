# R&D Career Radar expansion research

## Summary

This research pass expanded the R&D Career Radar across organization sizes and sectors while preserving the inclusion rule: public evidence of meaningful R&D activity, viability for someone living in Curitiba, and a recurring institutional opportunity channel.

Nineteen organizations were promoted during these expansion passes: Renault Group / Renault do Brasil, HORSE Powertrain Brasil, Mondelēz International, Electrolux Group, Novonesis, ERT Bioplastics, LG Electronics, Herbarium, IDR-Paraná, Copel, Sanepar, Lightera, Bosch Curitiba, Valmet, Arteche, SiDi, Siderquímica, Solabia Group / Laborclin, and Instron Brasil / EMIC.

Acceptance decisions use primary sources. Candidates remain in `To investigate` whenever R&D relevance, geographic viability, or the recurring opportunity channel is not sufficiently established.

## Findings

### Pass 1: industrial, consumer, biotech, and deeptech organizations

- **Renault Group / Renault do Brasil — Accept.** The Ayrton Senna complex in São José dos Pinhais hosts the Renault Design Center LatAm, which develops concepts and models for Latin America and contributes to global projects. The group maintains a recurring careers portal. [Renault Design Center LatAm](https://media.renaultgroup.com/renault-group-inaugurates-its-brand-new-renault-design-center-latam/) [Renault Careers](https://www.renaultgroup.com/en/careers/)
- **HORSE Powertrain Brasil — Accept.** HORSE identifies Curitiba as a plant and R&D center and maintains a recurring jobs portal. [HORSE Brazil](https://horse-powertrain.com/documents-brazil) [HORSE Jobs](https://jobs.horse-powertrain.com/pt-BR)
- **Mondelēz International — Accept.** Mondelēz identifies a global Technology Center in Curitiba and a formal Research & Development career function covering product, process, packaging and quality work. [Mondelēz Brazil](https://www.mondelezinternational.com/brazil/) [Mondelēz R&D](https://www.mondelezinternational.com/careers/career-areas/research-development/)
- **Electrolux Group — Accept.** Electrolux careers material connects Product Engineering and recurring product-development, electronics/software and laboratory roles to Curitiba. [Electrolux teams](https://career.electroluxgroup.com/global/en/meet-our-teams) [Electrolux Careers](https://career.electroluxgroup.com/global/en/)
- **Novonesis — Accept.** Novonesis defines R&D as the basis of its biosolutions, lists operations in Araucária and Quatro Barras, and maintains a recurring jobs portal. [Novonesis R&D](https://www.novonesis.com/pt/careers/areas-of-work/research-and-development) [Novonesis subsidiaries](https://www.novonesis.com/pt/about-us/subsidiaries) [Novonesis Jobs](https://www.novonesis.com/pt/careers/jobs)
- **ERT Bioplastics — Accept.** ERT traces its technology to academic polymer-engineering research, operates in Curitiba, and explicitly lists Engenharia e P&D on its recurring careers page. [ERT Sobre](https://ertbio.com/sobre/) [ERT Trabalhe Conosco](https://ertbio.com/trabalhe-conosco/)
- **LG Electronics — Accept.** LG's August 2026 Paraná announcement states that its Fazenda Rio Grande plant includes a dedicated R&D nucleus with about 40 professionals. [LG Paraná plant](https://www.lg.com/br/sobre-a-lg/press-media/lg-inaugura-fabrica-de-geladeiras-no-parana/) [LG Careers](https://www.lg.com/br/sobre-a-lg/trabalhe-conosco/)

- **Herbarium — Accept.** Herbarium maintains a specialized PD&I area and the Programa Herbarium de Inovação, collaborates with ICTs, and has recurring careers in Colombo. [Herbarium inovação](https://herbarium.com.br/parceria-para-inovacao/) [Herbarium carreiras](https://herbarium.com.br/trabalheconosco/)
- **IDR-Paraná — Accept.** The institute has formal Pesquisa, Desenvolvimento e Inovação activities, a research pole in Curitiba, and a recurring Bolsa Técnica page under its Diretoria de Pesquisa e Inovação. [IDR PDI](https://www.idrparana.pr.gov.br/Pagina/Pesquisa-Desenvolvimento-e-Inovacao-no-IDR-Parana) [IDR Bolsa Técnica](https://www.idrparana.pr.gov.br/Pagina/Bolsa-Tecnica)
- **Copel — Accept.** Copel maintains a formal ANEEL-regulated Programa de Pesquisa, Desenvolvimento e Inovação and a recurring careers page with Curitiba opportunities. [Copel P&D+i](https://www.copel.com/site/institucional/programa-de-pesquisa-e-desenvolvimento/) [Copel Carreira](https://www.copel.com/site/institucional/carreira/)
- **Sanepar — Accept.** Sanepar describes P&D+i as a continuous strategic practice, maintains a Gerência de Pesquisa e Inovação with dedicated staff, is headquartered in Curitiba, and hires through a recurring concursos page. [Sanepar P&D](https://www.sanepar.com.br/pesquisa-desenvolvimento) [Sanepar inovação](https://www.sanepar.com.br/inovacao-e-sustentabilidade) [Sanepar concursos](https://www.sanepar.com.br/concursos)
- **Lightera — Accept.** Lightera reports more than 100 R&D professionals, maintains global R&D in optical technologies, records a research laboratory in Curitiba in its institutional history, and has active Curitiba facilities plus a recurring careers page. [Lightera inovação](https://lightera.com/pt-br/inovacao/) [Lightera history](https://lightera.com/about-us/lightera-latam-history/) [Lightera careers](https://lightera.com/pt-br/pessoas-e-carreiras/)

### Pass 3: engineering, energy technology, and remote Brazil

- **Bosch Curitiba — Accept.** Bosch lists Research and Development as a formal career field and, in September 2026, published an official onsite Curitiba role in product-development engineering. [Bosch work fields](https://www.bosch.com.br/carreiras/campos-de-trabalho/) [Bosch Curitiba product-development role](https://jobs.smartrecruiters.com/BoschGroup/744000147586229-engenharia-de-desenvolvimento-de-produto-pl-foco-em-fluidos-e-termica-35734-) [Bosch Careers](https://www.bosch.com.br/carreiras/)
- **Valmet — Accept.** Valmet reports more than 500 R&D professionals and dozens of R&D facilities globally, identifies Araucária as its South American base, and maintains recurring Brazilian careers and open-job channels. [Valmet R&D](https://www.valmet.com/pt/sobre-a-valmet/sobre/pesquisa-e-desenvolvimento/) [Valmet Brazil](https://www.valmet.com/pt/sobre-a-valmet/) [Valmet Careers](https://www.valmet.com/pt/carreira-valmet/)
- **Arteche — Accept.** Arteche's current careers portal states that the group has seven R&D&I centers and shows active hybrid opportunities in Curitiba, establishing both company-level R&D and local employment viability. [Arteche Careers](https://careers.arteche.com/)
- **SiDi — Accept as remote-brazil.** SiDi describes its services as research, development and innovation from initial research through prototyping and application. Its careers page says more than 800 Sidiers are spread across Brazil and includes home-office support. [SiDi Services](https://www.sidi.org.br/pt-br/servicos) [SiDi Careers](https://www.sidi.org.br/pt-br/carreiras)

### Pass 4: mid-sized chemistry, diagnostics, and scientific instrumentation

- **Siderquímica — Accept.** The company's institutional site in São José dos Pinhais explicitly identifies an R&D Department and states that it develops chemical technologies for pulp, paper, wood panels and textile dyeing. It also maintains a recurring `Trabalhe Conosco` page. [Siderquímica](https://siderquimica.com.br/) [Siderquímica careers](https://siderquimica.com.br/trabalhe-conosco/)
- **Solabia Group / Laborclin — Accept.** Solabia states that it maintains seven complementary R&D centers and lists its Laborclin site in Curitiba for diagnostic and microbiological-analysis products. The group also maintains a recurring global careers page. [Solabia know-how](https://www.solabia.com/our-know-how/) [Solabia careers](https://www.solabia.com/careers/)
- **Instron Brasil / EMIC — Accept.** Instron's first-party EMIC material documents investments in research and development, describes a South American team of engineers, technicians and researchers, and explains that the EMIC line is developed and manufactured in São José dos Pinhais. Instron also has a recurring ITW careers portal. [Instron EMIC Series 23](https://www.instron.com/es-ar/-/media/literature-library/products/2018/01/2300-series-brazil-brochure.pdf) [Instron locations](https://www.instron.com/pt-br/our-company/about-us/locations/) [Instron careers](https://careers.itw.com/us/en/instron)

## Gaps and caveats

- **CPQD:** strong R&D evidence and recurring careers, but current public evidence still does not establish remote-Brazil eligibility strongly enough for someone living in Curitiba. [CPQD](https://www.cpqd.com.br/sobre-o-cpqd/)
- **Instituto Atlântico:** explicit R&D activity and recurring careers are established, but the current institutional material does not clearly prove fully remote Brazil eligibility. [Atlântico Careers](https://www.atlantico.com.br/trabalhe-conosco/)
- **Fundação CERTI:** excellent PD&I and scholarship opportunities, but the organization describes a hybrid model around locations outside the RMC rather than remote-Brazil eligibility. [CERTI Trabalhe Conosco](https://certi.org.br/trabalhe-conosco/)
- **Human Robotics:** Curitiba presence and a careers channel are known, but explicit institutional R&D/PD&I evidence remains weaker than the accepted catalog threshold.
- **Microcapsules Tech:** strong local biotech/deeptech R&D evidence exists, but a stable recurring institutional careers/jobs channel still needs verification.
- **Neodent:** Curitiba headquarters and a strong product-innovation ecosystem are established, but the current first-party evidence chain for explicit R&D plus recurring careers is still incomplete.
- **BEAUTYCOLOR Company:** Pinhais presence and a recurring careers channel are established, but first-party P&D evidence remains insufficient.
- **Pumatronix:** the company explicitly describes long-running R&D and its own technology laboratory in Curitiba, but no stable recurring careers/jobs channel was verified. [Pumatronix](https://pumatronix.com/quem-somos/)
- **Perkons:** Pinhais headquarters and technology development are established, but explicit institutional R&D framing remains weaker than the accepted threshold.
