# R&D Career Radar expansion research

## Summary

This research pass expanded the R&D Career Radar across organization sizes and sectors while keeping the existing inclusion rule: public evidence of meaningful R&D activity, viability for someone living in Curitiba, and a recurring institutional opportunity channel.

The first expansion promoted Renault Group / Renault do Brasil, HORSE Powertrain Brasil, Mondelēz International, Electrolux Group, Novonesis, ERT Bioplastics, and LG Electronics.

A second pass promoted Herbarium, Instituto de Desenvolvimento Rural do Paraná (IDR-Paraná), Copel, Sanepar, and Lightera. These additions broaden the catalog into pharmaceuticals and phytotherapy, public agricultural research, energy, sanitation, and optical communications.

Candidates stay outside the accepted catalog when one link in the evidence chain remains weak, even when the organization is technologically relevant.

## Findings

### Renault Group / Renault do Brasil

- **Fact:** Renault Group operates the Ayrton Senna complex in São José dos Pinhais and its current Curitiba plant page describes continued investment in the site and expansion of the Renault Design Center LatAm. [Renault Group, Curitiba plant](https://www.renaultgroup.com/en/group/locations/curitiba-plant/)
- **Fact:** Renault Group states that the Renault Design Center LatAm at the Ayrton Senna complex develops concepts and models for Brazil and Latin America and contributes to global Renault projects. [Renault Group Media, Renault Design Center LatAm](https://media.renaultgroup.com/renault-group-inaugurates-its-brand-new-renault-design-center-latam/)
- **Fact:** Renault Group maintains a recurring careers portal. [Renault Group Careers](https://www.renaultgroup.com/en/careers/)
- **Decision:** Accept. The local product-development/design capability is explicit and the site is in the RMC.

### HORSE Powertrain Brasil

- **Fact:** HORSE identifies its Curitiba operation as a plant and R&D center in its official Brazil documentation. [HORSE, Documents Brazil](https://horse-powertrain.com/documents-brazil)
- **Fact:** HORSE maintains a recurring jobs portal with Brazil as a supported career market. [HORSE Jobs](https://jobs.horse-powertrain.com/pt-BR)
- **Decision:** Accept. Local R&D is directly stated by the organization.

### Mondelēz International

- **Fact:** Mondelēz states that one of its global Technology Centers is located in Curitiba and identifies Pesquisa, Desenvolvimento, Qualidade e Inovação as a career area in Brazil. [Mondelēz Brazil](https://www.mondelezinternational.com/brazil/)
- **Fact:** The company describes a global Research & Development function spanning research, product, process, packaging, quality, and related disciplines. [Mondelēz R&D Careers](https://www.mondelezinternational.com/careers/career-areas/research-development/)
- **Fact:** Mondelēz maintains a recurring global careers portal. [Mondelēz Careers](https://www.mondelezinternational.com/careers/)
- **Decision:** Accept. The Curitiba Technology Center is an explicit local R&D anchor.

### Electrolux Group

- **Fact:** Electrolux Group's careers material identifies Product Engineering leadership in Curitiba and includes Research & Development as a formal career area. [Electrolux Group, Meet our teams](https://career.electroluxgroup.com/global/en/meet-our-teams)
- **Fact:** The official careers site has published Curitiba roles in product development, electronics/software development, and laboratory work, showing an active local R&D employment path. [Electrolux Group Careers](https://career.electroluxgroup.com/global/en/)
- **Decision:** Accept. The evidence is career-oriented rather than a standalone corporate R&D-center page, but it directly ties R&D/product engineering work to Curitiba.

### Novonesis

- **Fact:** Novonesis defines Research & Development as the starting point for its biosolutions and lists applied research plus product and process development among its R&D disciplines. [Novonesis R&D Careers](https://www.novonesis.com/pt/careers/areas-of-work/research-and-development)
- **Fact:** Novonesis lists legal operations in Araucária and Quatro Barras, both in the Curitiba metropolitan area. [Novonesis subsidiaries](https://www.novonesis.com/pt/about-us/subsidiaries)
- **Fact:** Novonesis maintains a recurring jobs portal. [Novonesis Jobs](https://www.novonesis.com/pt/careers/jobs)
- **Decision:** Accept. The organization-level R&D capability and RMC presence are both first-party verified, although the static pages do not enumerate a local R&D org chart.

### ERT Bioplastics

- **Fact:** ERT states that its technology originated in an academic research unit focused on polymer engineering and compostable bioplastics and that it established an industrial operation in Curitiba. [ERT Bioplastics, Sobre](https://ertbio.com/sobre/)
- **Fact:** Its recurring careers page explicitly includes Engenharia e P&D among the areas for candidates. [ERT Bioplastics, Trabalhe Conosco](https://ertbio.com/trabalhe-conosco/)
- **Decision:** Accept. This is a strong local deeptech/materials example with both technical provenance and a recurring opportunity channel.

### LG Electronics

- **Fact:** In August 2026 LG announced a new refrigerator plant in Fazenda Rio Grande and stated that the site includes a dedicated Research & Development nucleus with about 40 professionals adapting global innovation to Brazilian needs. [LG Electronics Brazil, Paraná plant](https://www.lg.com/br/sobre-a-lg/press-media/lg-inaugura-fabrica-de-geladeiras-no-parana/)
- **Fact:** LG maintains a recurring Brazilian careers page. [LG Electronics Brazil, Trabalhe Conosco](https://www.lg.com/br/sobre-a-lg/trabalhe-conosco/)
- **Decision:** Accept. This is unusually direct and current evidence of a new local R&D operation in the RMC.

## Second pass

### Herbarium

- **Fact:** Herbarium states that it maintains a specialized Regulatória e de PD&I area and the Programa Herbarium de Inovação, including collaboration with ICTs around phytotherapy innovation. [Herbarium, Parceria para inovação](https://herbarium.com.br/parceria-para-inovacao/)
- **Fact:** Its recurring careers page describes the company as a reference in fitotherapy with excellence in research and development. [Herbarium, Trabalhe Conosco](https://herbarium.com.br/trabalheconosco/)
- **Fact:** Current institutional hiring material places roles, including PDI opportunities, in Colombo in the Curitiba metropolitan area. [Herbarium, Programa de Estágio](https://herbarium.com.br/noticias/herbarium-abre-processo-seletivo-para-programa-de-estagio-no-parana/)
- **Decision:** Accept.

### Instituto de Desenvolvimento Rural do Paraná (IDR-Paraná)

- **Fact:** IDR-Paraná has a dedicated institutional page for Pesquisa, Desenvolvimento e Inovação and defines technology development objectives for agriculture and rural development. [IDR-Paraná, PDI](https://www.idrparana.pr.gov.br/Pagina/Pesquisa-Desenvolvimento-e-Inovacao-no-IDR-Parana)
- **Fact:** The institute maintains a recurring Bolsa Técnica page under its Diretoria de Pesquisa e Inovação, with repeated 2026 selections for research projects and innovation activities. [IDR-Paraná, Bolsa Técnica](https://www.idrparana.pr.gov.br/Pagina/Bolsa-Tecnica)
- **Fact:** IDR-Paraná maintains a regional research pole in Curitiba. [IDR-Paraná, Polo Regional de Pesquisa de Curitiba](https://www.idrparana.pr.gov.br/Endereco/Polo-Regional-de-Pesquisa-de-Curitiba)
- **Decision:** Accept. Scholarships are explicitly inside the Career Radar's approved opportunity scope.

### Copel

- **Fact:** Copel maintains a formal Programa de Pesquisa, Desenvolvimento e Inovação regulated by ANEEL and describes technology-development objectives for the electricity sector. [Copel, Programa de P&D+i](https://www.copel.com/site/institucional/programa-de-pesquisa-e-desenvolvimento/)
- **Fact:** The P&D+i page lists a Curitiba institutional address. [Copel, Programa de P&D+i](https://www.copel.com/site/institucional/programa-de-pesquisa-e-desenvolvimento/)
- **Fact:** Copel maintains a recurring careers page with current Curitiba positions in technical and engineering fields. [Copel, Carreira](https://www.copel.com/site/institucional/carreira/)
- **Decision:** Accept.

### Sanepar

- **Fact:** Sanepar defines P&D+i as a continuous and strategic practice connecting science, technology, sustainability, universities, startups, and technology companies. [Sanepar, Pesquisa e Desenvolvimento](https://www.sanepar.com.br/pesquisa-desenvolvimento)
- **Fact:** Its innovation governance includes a Gerência de Pesquisa e Inovação staffed by employees, including masters and PhDs, dedicated full-time to P&D+i activities. [Sanepar, Inovação e Sustentabilidade](https://www.sanepar.com.br/inovacao-e-sustentabilidade)
- **Fact:** Sanepar is headquartered in Curitiba and maintains a recurring public-concurso page for hiring. [Sanepar, Concursos](https://www.sanepar.com.br/concursos)
- **Decision:** Accept.

### Lightera

- **Fact:** Lightera states that it has more than 100 professionals dedicated to R&D across optical technologies, hardware, software, photonics and systems research. [Lightera, Inovação](https://lightera.com/pt-br/inovacao/)
- **Fact:** Lightera's institutional history records the opening of a research laboratory in Curitiba, and its current footprint includes production facilities and an office in Curitiba. [Lightera LatAm history](https://lightera.com/about-us/lightera-latam-history/) [Lightera, About](https://lightera.com/about-us/)
- **Fact:** Its recurring people-and-careers page explicitly references the Curitiba plant and links to available jobs. [Lightera, Pessoas e Carreiras](https://lightera.com/pt-br/pessoas-e-carreiras/)
- **Decision:** Accept.

## Gaps and caveats

### Bosch Curitiba

- **Verified:** Bosch has a Curitiba site and Research & Development is a formal Bosch career field. [Bosch Curitiba](https://www.bosch.com.br/nossa-empresa/bosch-no-brasil/curitiba/) [Bosch work fields](https://www.bosch.com.br/carreiras/campos-de-trabalho/)
- **Gap:** Current first-party evidence tying substantial R&D activity specifically to the Curitiba site is still weaker and less direct than for accepted organizations.
- **Status:** Keep in `To investigate`.

### Neodent

- **Verified:** Neodent is headquartered in Curitiba and its institutional material emphasizes product innovation and advanced dental technology. [Neodent](https://neodent.com.br/) [Neodent, Sobre nós](https://neodent.com.br/pacientes/sobre-nos/)
- **Gap:** A stable first-party careers route and a sufficiently explicit institutional P&D description were not both established in this pass.
- **Status:** Keep in `To investigate`.

### BEAUTYCOLOR Company

- **Verified:** BEAUTYCOLOR has an institutional presence in Pinhais and its contact page exposes a recurring `Trabalhe Conosco` channel. [BEAUTYCOLOR, Contato](https://beautycolor.com.br/contato)
- **Gap:** Current evidence for P&D roles was discoverable outside first-party sources, which does not meet the evidence standard for accepted records in this research pass.
- **Status:** Keep in `To investigate`.

### Pumatronix

- **Verified:** Pumatronix states that it has spent more than 18 years on research and development of intelligent transport systems and maintains its own technology laboratory in Curitiba. [Pumatronix, Quem somos](https://pumatronix.com/quem-somos/)
- **Gap:** No stable recurring institutional careers/jobs channel was verified in this pass.
- **Status:** Keep in `To investigate`.

### Instituto Atlântico

- **Verified:** Instituto Atlântico has explicit R&D/PD&I activity and a recurring careers portal. [Instituto Atlântico](https://www.atlantico.com.br/) [Trabalhe Conosco](https://www.atlantico.com.br/trabalhe-conosco/)
- **Gap:** Its base is outside Curitiba/RMC and the current institutional material did not explicitly establish fully remote Brazil eligibility for R&D positions.
- **Status:** Keep in `To investigate`.

### Arteche

- **Verified:** Arteche has global R&D&I capability and a recurring careers portal with Brazil opportunities. [Arteche Careers](https://careers.arteche.com/)

- **Status:** Keep in `To investigate`.

### Valmet

- **Gap:** A current first-party source tying R&D employment specifically to the Araucária operation was not established.
- **Status:** Keep in `To investigate`.

### Perkons

- **Verified:** Perkons is headquartered in Pinhais, develops traffic technology and maintains recurring institutional contact channels. [Perkons](https://www.perkons.com/sobre/institucional/) [Contato](https://www.perkons.com/contato/fale-conosco/)
