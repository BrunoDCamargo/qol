# R&D Career Radar: comprehensive follow-up

Date: 2026-09-14

## Scope

This follow-up continues the sector-agnostic search defined in `career-radar/FOCUS.md`. It uses the same three gates: recurring R&D/research/development activity, practical viability while living in Curitiba, and a recurring institutional path into the work.

## Promoted: Curitiba and RMC

### AGP sGlass

**Verified facts**

- AGP's current Brazil plant page places the operation in São José dos Pinhais and states that the plant develops advanced transparent-armor products, maintains a ballistic laboratory and invests more than 10% of net sales in R&D and new technologies. [AGP Brazil plant](https://agpglass.com/bra/sobre-sglass/)
- AGP maintains `Pesquisa & Desenvolvimento` as a formal career team. Its careers material describes R&D as creating new solutions and innovative processes and integrating R&D with production and new-model launches. [AGP teams](https://agpglass.com/equipes/)
- The official careers area provides an institutional application route. [AGP careers](https://agpglass.com/nossa-cultura/)

**Decision:** promote as a primary local materials, automotive-glass and advanced-product R&D target.

### Protium Dynamics

**Verified facts**

- Protium Dynamics is headquartered in Curitiba and describes itself as a technology-based company dedicated to hydrogen solutions, with a multidisciplinary team specialized in research, development and engineering. [Protium Dynamics](https://protiumdynamics.com/)
- Its institutional PD&I page describes structured research and development of hydrogen, Power-to-X, industrial data/AI and custom technology solutions. [Pesquisa, Desenvolvimento e Inovação](https://protiumdynamics.com/pesquisa-desenvolvimento-e-inovacao/)
- IEL Paraná currently maintains multiple doctoral-researcher selections for Protium Dynamics under the `Doutores Inovando no Setor Empresarial` program. The positions are hybrid in Curitiba and cover experimental work, ML/data science, industrial emissions, digital infrastructure and technical-scientific publication. [Pesquisador Doutorado 27850](https://carreiras.iel.org.br/PR/vaga/bolsista/27850/pesquisador-doutorado-protium-dynamics-doutores-inovando-no-setor-empresaria-iel-pr) [Pesquisador Doutorado 27852](https://carreiras.iel.org.br/PR/vaga/bolsista/27852/pesquisador-doutorado-protium-dynamics-doutores-inovando-no-setor-empresaria-iel-pr)

**Decision:** promote as a primary Curitiba/hybrid hydrogen, energy-transition and applied-R&D target. The recurring path currently includes research fellowships rather than only conventional employment, which is explicitly allowed by the radar scope.

### Toroid do Brasil

**Verified facts**

- Toroid do Brasil has operated in São José dos Pinhais since 1994 and manufactures transformers and inductive components. It maintains an institutional `Trabalhe Conosco` route. [Toroid](https://toroid.com.br/) [Trabalhe Conosco](https://toroid.com.br/trabalhe-conosco/)
- IEL Paraná currently offers a doctoral researcher position embedded at Toroid for a strategic program in process innovation and development of new toroidal and medium-voltage transformer products, including prototyping and validation. [Pesquisador Doutor - Toroid](https://carreiras.iel.org.br/PR/vaga/bolsista/28050/pesquisador-doutor-toroid-do-brasil-doutores-inovando-no-setor-empresarial-iel-pr)

**Decision:** promote as a primary local electrical-engineering, power-electronics and product-development R&D target.
