# R&D Career Radar expansion: AI and clinical research remote-Brazil pass

## Summary

This pass looked for additional R&D-oriented organizations with current, primary-source evidence that someone living in Curitiba can participate without relocating. Three organizations pass the catalog rule: Blip, AE Studio, and ICON plc.

Fortrea, Worldwide Clinical Trials, and Medpace were reviewed but are not promoted in this pass because current public evidence did not establish remote-Brazil viability as cleanly as the accepted candidates.

## Accepted organizations

### Blip

**Decision: Accept as `remote-brazil`.**

- Blip's employer-controlled Greenhouse board currently lists positions as `Remote - Brazil`, establishing current geographic viability. [Blip jobs](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/blip-global)
- A current `Staff AI Engineer` role explicitly describes the position as an AI engineer/researcher leading applied research across the machine-learning lifecycle, with emphasis on language models, model creation, fine-tuning and distillation. [Staff AI Engineer](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/blip-global/jobs/4324655009)
- The recurring jobs board contains multiple Brazil-remote roles and job alerts, so this is not a single isolated posting. [Blip jobs](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/blip-global)

### AE Studio

**Decision: Accept as `remote-brazil`.**

- AE Studio's recurring Greenhouse board has a dedicated `Data Science & Research` area and a current `Applied AI Data Scientist (Brazil)` opening listed as `Florianopolis office/ Brazil remote`. [AE Studio jobs](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/aestudio)
- The Brazil role states that employees work on client projects and internal research initiatives and can contribute to AI alignment research. [Applied AI Data Scientist (Brazil)](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/aestudio/jobs/4011417004)
- AE Studio also maintains a research team dedicated to AI safety/alignment R&D and publishes current research roles, strengthening the organization-level research classification. [Alignment Scientist/Engineer](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/aestudio/jobs/6009528004)

### ICON plc

**Decision: Accept as `remote-brazil`.**

- ICON identifies itself as a healthcare intelligence and clinical research organization working on clinical development and the advancement of medicines and devices. [ICON Brazil](https://careers.iconplc.com/jobs-in-brazil)
- Its Brazil careers page currently lists multiple roles with `Remote` or `Remote or Office` working arrangements. [ICON Brazil](https://careers.iconplc.com/jobs-in-brazil)
- A current Clinical Trial Manager role explicitly says `Remote - Brazil`; current CRA and Clinical Associate roles are also marked remote. [Clinical Trial Manager](https://careers.iconplc.com/job/clinical-trial-manager-and-sr-clinical-trial-manager-in-brazil-sao-paulo-jid-53266) [Clinical Associate](https://careers.iconplc.com/job/clinical-associate-in-brazil-sao-paulo-jid-53703)

## Investigated but not promoted

### Fortrea

- Fortrea clearly qualifies as a clinical-development/CRO organization and has a recurring first-party careers portal. [Fortrea Careers](https://careers.fortrea.com/us/en)
- The current first-party material reviewed in this pass did not expose a Brazil-specific remote role strongly enough to establish present Curitiba viability. Keep as a future candidate rather than inferring remote eligibility from global flexibility.

### Worldwide Clinical Trials

- Worldwide operates a full-service CRO office in São Paulo and performs drug-development and clinical-research work across multiple therapeutic areas. [Worldwide Brazil](https://www.worldwide.com/global-reach/sao-paulo-cro/)
- The reviewed primary material establishes Brazil operations and careers, but not current remote-Brazil eligibility. Keep as a future candidate.

### Medpace

- Medpace is a full-service CRO with current clinical-research openings in São Paulo and a recurring careers portal. [Medpace CRA](https://careers.medpace.com/jobs/12814?lang=en-us) [Medpace Careers](https://careers.medpace.com/jobs)
- Current Brazil roles reviewed are office-based or require substantial field activity, and the careers material describes an office-based culture with only occasional WFH for eligible positions. This does not satisfy the Curitiba viability rule.

## Recommendation

Add Blip, AE Studio, and ICON plc to `career-radar/organizations.yaml` and the `Remote in Brazil` table. Keep Fortrea and Worldwide Clinical Trials as future investigation targets. Do not add Medpace unless a genuinely remote-Brazil role appears.
