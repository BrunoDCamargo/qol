# Which new R&D career targets should be added to the Curitiba Career Radar on 2026-09-15?

## Summary

This research pass identified seven new additions that satisfy the current Career Radar rule strongly enough to preserve in a verified expansion batch:

- Intecso Soluções e Inovações em Agronegócio
- M. Dias Branco / Latinex Brands
- FORVIA Faurecia
- Vidya Technology
- BMR Medical
- Tether Data
- CloudWalk

Five are Curitiba/RMC targets and two are current remote-Brazil targets. Tether Data is the clearest remote addition because its current careers site lists multiple AI Research Engineer roles as 100% remote worldwide and explicitly includes São Paulo, Brazil among eligible locations. CloudWalk combines an established internal AI R&D organization with current fully remote machine-learning hiring.

Mondelēz International / Mondelēz Brasil was also revalidated in this pass, but `mondelez-curitiba` already exists in the consolidated `organizations.yaml`, so it is not duplicated in this expansion batch.

Latinex is retained with a caveat: the local São José dos Pinhais P&D role is currently evidenced through a secondary listing, while the parent company's research environment and recurring careers path are first-party.

## Findings

### Intecso Soluções e Inovações em Agronegócio

Verified fact: Intecso describes itself as specialized in development of technologies and laboratory analyses. Its Curitiba laboratory performs physical, chemical and biological analyses and explicitly offers development and validation of analytical methods and process optimization.

Verified fact: employer-authored recruiting posts show repeated Curitiba hiring for technical development work, including Analista de Desenvolvimento Analítico, Analista em Bioinformática and P&D fellowship opportunities.

Disposition: promote as a local applied-R&D / analytical-development target.

### Mondelēz International / Mondelēz Brasil

Verified fact: Mondelēz's official Brazil careers page states that one of the company's global Technology Centers is located in Curitiba and explicitly describes employment opportunities there in Pesquisa, Desenvolvimento, Qualidade e Inovação, focused on improving products, ingredients and processes.

Verified fact: Mondelēz maintains a global R&D career function and recurring careers/search infrastructure.

Disposition: revalidated as a high-confidence local food/product R&D target, but no new expansion entry is added because `mondelez-curitiba` is already present in the consolidated registry.

### M. Dias Branco / Latinex Brands

Verified fact: M. Dias Branco documents an ongoing innovation structure that includes Germinar Pesquisa, connecting the company with universities, research centers, deeptechs and researchers to solve industrial challenges at TRL 3-7. The company also maintains a recurring corporate careers portal.

Secondary current evidence: a current `Analista de Pesquisa e Desenvolvimento` posting for Time Latinex Brands in São José dos Pinhais describes development of products, processes and packaging, laboratory and industrial tests, innovation and technical documentation.

Disposition: promote, but preserve the secondary-evidence caveat until the local role can be tied to a first-party source.

### FORVIA Faurecia

Verified fact: FORVIA's official careers material has a dedicated R&D track and states that its R&D teams develop products, materials and solutions for future vehicles. Its global-presence directory lists a Quatro Barras site in Paraná.

Current local research evidence: IEL Paraná is recruiting doctoral researchers for Faurecia projects in Quatro Barras involving artificial intelligence, industrial data, IoT, human-machine interfaces, process optimization and Industry 4.0. The current project is hybrid with three days per week on site.

Disposition: promote as a local automotive / industrial-R&D target.

### Vidya Technology

Verified fact: Vidya's official site states that the company has more than a decade of R&D experience and lists research programs with Petrobras, CENPES, ANP, Tecnova and SENAI across corrosion monitoring, AI and digital-twin technologies.

Verified fact: the company identifies Curitiba as its Brazilian office and maintains a dedicated recruiting portal with technology openings and a standing local talent pool.

Disposition: promote as a high-confidence Curitiba deeptech / industrial-AI R&D target.

### BMR Medical

Verified fact: BMR Medical's official site identifies its manufacturing plant in Campina Grande do Sul and describes the company as a developer and manufacturer of health products and medical devices. It maintains a recurring official career page.

Current research evidence: IEL Paraná has multiple doctoral innovation opportunities at BMR Medical covering intelligent automation of medical-device production, integrated logistics, manufacturing optimization and implementation of a regulated pharmaceutical production line.

Disposition: promote as a local medical-device / health-manufacturing applied-R&D target.

### Tether Data

Verified fact: Tether Data's current careers site explicitly describes an AI research environment and lists multiple AI Research Engineer roles covering LLM and multimodal pre-training, reinforcement learning and agentic post-training.

Verified fact: current `100% Remote Worldwide` research-engineer postings include São Paulo, Brazil among eligible locations, establishing present remote-Brazil viability rather than relying on a historical remote policy.

Disposition: promote as a high-confidence remote-Brazil frontier-AI research target.

### CloudWalk

Verified fact: CloudWalk's own material describes an internal AI R&D organization and research programs. Its 2025 research-residency reporting states that more than sixty people worked in its internal research and development team focused on intensive AI-model exploration.

Verified current fact: CloudWalk currently recruits a fully remote Machine Learning Engineer to design, train and deploy ML models in its production security systems, and its careers material describes remote engineering roles and AI-native product development.

Disposition: promote as a remote-Brazil applied-AI / fintech R&D target.

### Candidates investigated but not promoted

PepsiCo has a substantial global R&D organization and a recurring careers platform that includes Curitiba as a hiring location. This pass did not locate enough evidence tying a durable R&D function specifically to the Curitiba operation.

ARAUCO has strong corporate research evidence through Bioforest and facilities in Araucária plus a corporate office in Curitiba. The evidence reviewed did not establish clearly enough that the RMC sites provide a recurring local R&D career path rather than operations, corporate and manufacturing roles.

Dataprom has a recurring Curitiba careers portal, local software and electronics development and a strong innovation narrative around intelligent mobility. The available first-party evidence still falls short of the radar's stricter research / experimentation threshold.

Coinbase currently has multiple Machine Learning positions explicitly listed as Remote - Brazil and a remote-first careers model. The roles involve multi-agent systems, RAG, fine-tuning and LLM evaluation, but the evidence reviewed describes a product-engineering environment more clearly than a durable research or R&D function. It remains a strong follow-up candidate rather than a promoted target in this batch.

Rede HDS continues to show a Curitiba role titled Pesquisa e Desenvolvimento, but the described work centers on product registration, drawing checks and support for new item development. It remains below the current promotion threshold.

A current confidential Curitiba P&D role in reactive polymers and resins appears technically strong, but the employer is not disclosed. It cannot be added until the organization can be identified and its recurring career path verified.

## Gaps and caveats

Intecso does not currently expose a conventional first-party careers portal on its website. Its recurring path is evidenced by repeated employer-authored LinkedIn recruiting and a stable recruiting channel, weaker than a dedicated ATS.

Latinex's current São José dos Pinhais P&D role was verified through a secondary job listing. The parent company's R&D environment and recurring careers channel are first-party.

FORVIA's current Quatro Barras research project is verified through IEL Paraná rather than FORVIA's own job board. The corporate R&D environment, local site and recurring careers channel are first-party.

BMR Medical's formal R&D signal comes from current IEL Paraná doctoral projects in addition to the company's own product-development and innovation material. Its long-term R&D organization is less explicitly documented than Mondelēz or Vidya.

CloudWalk's current remote ML role is clear, while the strongest explicit description of the size of its internal R&D team comes from company reporting published in 2025. Current company pages still show active AI research initiatives, but later passes should keep checking the durability of the formal research organization.
