# Which new R&D career targets should be added to the Curitiba Career Radar on 2026-09-15?

## Summary

This research pass identified three additions that satisfy the current Career Radar rule strongly enough to preserve in a verified expansion batch:

- Intecso Soluções e Inovações em Agronegócio
- Mondelēz International / Mondelēz Brasil
- M. Dias Branco / Latinex Brands

Mondelēz is the strongest addition in this pass because its own Brazil careers page explicitly identifies Curitiba as one of its global Technology Centers and states that Research, Development, Quality and Innovation careers are available there.

Intecso has direct first-party evidence of analytical technology development in Curitiba and repeated employer-authored recruiting for analytical development, bioinformatics and P&D fellowships.

Latinex was retained because the main unresolved gap from the previous pass is now narrower: a current P&D role in São José dos Pinhais provides location-specific evidence, while parent company M. Dias Branco documents an established research and innovation structure and maintains a recurring corporate careers channel. The location-specific vacancy evidence remains secondary rather than first-party, so this entry should be rechecked during later consolidation.

## Findings

### Intecso Soluções e Inovações em Agronegócio

Verified fact: Intecso describes itself as specialized in development of technologies and laboratory analyses. Its Curitiba laboratory performs physical, chemical and biological analyses and explicitly offers development and validation of analytical methods and process optimization.

Verified fact: employer-authored recruiting posts show repeated Curitiba hiring for technical development work, including Analista de Desenvolvimento Analítico, Analista em Bioinformática and P&D fellowship opportunities. The postings use the company's recruiting address and identify Curitiba as the work location.

Disposition: promote as a local applied-R&D / analytical-development target.

### Mondelēz International / Mondelēz Brasil

Verified fact: Mondelēz's official Brazil careers page states that one of the company's global Technology Centers is located in Curitiba and explicitly describes employment opportunities there in Pesquisa, Desenvolvimento, Qualidade e Inovação, focused on improving products, ingredients and processes.

Verified fact: Mondelēz maintains a global R&D career function and recurring careers/search infrastructure. Its R&D pages describe product development, packaging, process development, engineering and technology across the company's R&D organization.

Disposition: promote as a high-confidence local food/product R&D target.

### M. Dias Branco / Latinex Brands

Verified fact: M. Dias Branco documents an ongoing innovation structure that includes Germinar Pesquisa, a program connecting the company with universities, research centers, deeptechs and researchers to solve industrial challenges at TRL 3-7. The company also documents a corporate history of research, development and innovation in products.

Verified fact: M. Dias Branco acquired Latinex in 2021 and maintains a recurring corporate careers portal.

Secondary current evidence: a current `Analista de Pesquisa e Desenvolvimento` posting for Time Latinex Brands in São José dos Pinhais describes development of products, processes and packaging, laboratory and industrial tests, innovation, technical documentation and interfaces with engineering and quality.

Disposition: promote, but preserve the secondary-evidence caveat until the location-specific P&D role can be tied to a first-party recruiting page or another first-party source.

## Gaps and caveats

Intecso does not currently expose a conventional first-party careers portal on its website. Its recurring path is evidenced by repeated employer-authored LinkedIn recruiting and a stable recruiting email. This is consistent with smaller research organizations but is weaker than a dedicated ATS.

Latinex's current São José dos Pinhais P&D role was verified through a secondary job listing. The parent company's R&D environment and recurring careers channel are first-party, but a later pass should seek a first-party copy of the Latinex role or another first-party source that ties the local unit directly to recurring P&D employment.

This pass prioritized strong local/RMC candidates and did not attempt another broad remote-Brazil sweep.
