# Which new R&D career targets should be added to the Curitiba Career Radar on 2026-09-15?

## Summary

This research pass identified six additions that satisfy the current Career Radar rule strongly enough to preserve in a verified expansion batch:

- Intecso Soluções e Inovações em Agronegócio
- Mondelēz International / Mondelēz Brasil
- M. Dias Branco / Latinex Brands
- FORVIA Faurecia
- Vidya Technology
- BMR Medical

Mondelēz remains one of the strongest additions because its own Brazil careers page identifies Curitiba as one of its global Technology Centers and states that Research, Development, Quality and Innovation careers are available there.

Vidya Technology is another high-confidence addition. Its own site documents more than a decade of R&D programs spanning artificial intelligence, digital twins, corrosion-monitoring hardware and spatial computing, while its current recruiting portal lists technology roles and a standing talent pool in Curitiba.

FORVIA Faurecia has both a current Quatro Barras site and a current doctoral R&D project there through IEL Paraná, focused on AI, IoT, industrial data and human-machine interfaces. BMR Medical has an RMC manufacturing site, a recurring careers channel and multiple current doctoral innovation projects involving automation, production, logistics and regulated health-product development.

Latinex was retained because the main unresolved gap from the previous pass is narrower: a current P&D role in São José dos Pinhais provides location-specific evidence, while parent company M. Dias Branco documents an established research and innovation structure and maintains a recurring corporate careers channel. The location-specific vacancy evidence remains secondary rather than first-party, so this entry should be rechecked during later consolidation.

## Findings

### Intecso Soluções e Inovações em Agronegócio

Verified fact: Intecso describes itself as specialized in development of technologies and laboratory analyses. Its Curitiba laboratory performs physical, chemical and biological analyses and explicitly offers development and validation of analytical methods and process optimization.

Verified fact: employer-authored recruiting posts show repeated Curitiba hiring for technical development work, including Analista de Desenvolvimento Analítico, Analista em Bioinformática and P&D fellowship opportunities. The postings use the company's recruiting address and identify Curitiba as the work location.

Disposition: promote as a local applied-R&D / analytical-development target.

### Mondelēz International / Mondelēz Brasil

Verified fact: Mondelēz's official Brazil careers page states that one of the company's global Technology Centers is located in Curitiba and explicitly describes employment opportunities there in Pesquisa, Desenvolvimento, Qualidade e Inovação, focused on improving products, ingredients and processes.

Verified fact: Mondelēz maintains a global R&D career function and recurring careers/search infrastructure. Its R&D pages describe product development, packaging, process development, engineering and technology across the company's R&D organization.

Disposition: promote as a high-confidence local food/product R&D target.

### M. Dias Branco / Latinex Brands

Verified fact: M. Dias Branco documents an ongoing innovation structure that includes Germinar Pesquisa, a program connecting the company with universities, research centers, deeptechs and researchers to solve industrial challenges at TRL 3-7. The company also documents a corporate history of research, development and innovation in products.

Verified fact: M. Dias Branco acquired Latinex in 2021 and maintains a recurring corporate careers portal.

Secondary current evidence: a current `Analista de Pesquisa e Desenvolvimento` posting for Time Latinex Brands in São José dos Pinhais describes development of products, processes and packaging, laboratory and industrial tests, innovation, technical documentation and interfaces with engineering and quality.

Disposition: promote, but preserve the secondary-evidence caveat until the location-specific P&D role can be tied to a first-party recruiting page or another first-party source.

### FORVIA Faurecia

Verified fact: FORVIA's official careers material has a dedicated R&D track and states that its R&D teams develop products, materials and solutions for future vehicles.

Verified fact: FORVIA's global-presence directory lists a Quatro Barras site in Paraná.

Current local research evidence: IEL Paraná is recruiting doctoral researchers for Faurecia projects in Quatro Barras involving artificial intelligence, industrial data, IoT, human-machine interfaces, process optimization and Industry 4.0. The current project is hybrid with three days per week on site.

Disposition: promote as a local automotive / industrial-R&D target.

### Vidya Technology

Verified fact: Vidya's official site states that the company has more than a decade of R&D experience and lists research programs with Petrobras, CENPES, ANP, Tecnova and SENAI across corrosion monitoring, AI and digital-twin technologies.

Verified fact: the company identifies Curitiba as its Brazilian office and maintains a dedicated recruiting portal. Current openings include software, data and spatial-computing roles, plus a standing talent pool in Curitiba.

Verified fact: Vidya also documents completed deeptech R&D projects with Petrobras covering AI-driven service automation, digital process safety and industrial corrosion-monitoring hardware.

Disposition: promote as a high-confidence Curitiba deeptech / industrial-AI R&D target.

### BMR Medical

Verified fact: BMR Medical's official site identifies its manufacturing plant in Campina Grande do Sul and describes the company as a developer and manufacturer of health products and medical devices for oncology and surgical applications.

Verified fact: BMR Medical maintains a recurring official career page with open-vacancy and curriculum channels.

Current research evidence: IEL Paraná has multiple doctoral innovation opportunities at BMR Medical. Current projects cover intelligent automation of medical-device production, integrated logistics, manufacturing optimization and implementation of a regulated line for non-sterile liquid medicines.

Disposition: promote as a local medical-device / health-manufacturing applied-R&D target.

### Candidates investigated but not promoted

PepsiCo has a substantial global R&D organization and a recurring careers platform that includes Curitiba as a hiring location. This pass did not locate enough evidence tying a durable R&D function specifically to the Curitiba operation, so it remains a candidate rather than a promoted target.

ARAUCO has strong corporate research evidence through Bioforest, including biotechnology, forestry, panels and industrial-process research, and it has facilities in Araucária plus a corporate office in Curitiba. The evidence reviewed did not establish clearly enough that the local RMC sites provide a recurring R&D career path rather than operations, corporate and manufacturing roles.

Dataprom has a recurring Curitiba careers portal, local software and electronics development, and a strong innovation narrative around intelligent mobility. The available first-party evidence still falls short of the Career Radar's stricter research / experimentation threshold.

Rede HDS continues to show a Curitiba role titled Pesquisa e Desenvolvimento, but the described work centers on product registration, drawing checks and support for new item development. It remains below the current promotion threshold.

A current confidential Curitiba P&D role in reactive polymers and resins appears technically strong, but the employer is not disclosed. It cannot be added until the organization can be identified and its recurring career path verified.

## Gaps and caveats

Intecso does not currently expose a conventional first-party careers portal on its website. Its recurring path is evidenced by repeated employer-authored LinkedIn recruiting and a stable recruiting email. This is consistent with smaller research organizations but is weaker than a dedicated ATS.

Latinex's current São José dos Pinhais P&D role was verified through a secondary job listing. The parent company's R&D environment and recurring careers channel are first-party, but a later pass should seek a first-party copy of the Latinex role or another first-party source that ties the local unit directly to recurring P&D employment.

FORVIA's current Quatro Barras research project is verified through IEL Paraná rather than FORVIA's own job board. The corporate R&D environment, local site and recurring careers channel are first-party.

BMR Medical's formal R&D signal comes from current IEL Paraná doctoral projects in addition to the company's own product-development and innovation materials. This is strong applied-research evidence, but its long-term R&D organization is less explicitly documented than Mondelēz or Vidya.

This pass prioritized strong local/RMC candidates. A separate remote-Brazil sweep would be useful because the repository gives local and remote-Brazil targets equal priority.
