# R&D Career Radar: local and remote-Brazil equal priority

## Summary

The Career Radar now treats two arrangements as equally important primary career targets:

1. sustained R&D careers available in Curitiba / RMC;
2. sustained R&D careers that can be performed remotely while living in Brazil.

Headquarters location does not determine priority. A remote-Brazil organization can be as relevant as a Curitiba organization when its R&D environment and career path are equally strong.

This pass also verified three additional primary targets: FIT Instituto de Tecnologia, Radix, and BairesDev.

## Scope decision

This is a project decision rather than an empirical claim. `career-radar/FOCUS.md` is the durable rule.

A candidate still needs all three gates:

- meaningful and recurring R&D / research / engineering-development activity;
- practical viability while living in Curitiba, including current remote-Brazil work;
- a recurring institutional career path.

Remote work is not a lower-priority fallback. It is one of the two main ways an organization can be viable from Curitiba.

## Verified additions

### FIT Instituto de Tecnologia

FIT describes itself as a nonprofit technology institute accredited for R&D activity and says it develops technologies in hardware, software and automation. Its institutional profile reports more than 500 professionals, more than R$450 million invested in R&D over five years, and more than 50 projects for global clients. [FIT — O Instituto](https://www.fit-tecnologia.org.br/wp/o-instituto/)

Its business areas explicitly include `Pesquisa e Desenvolvimento`, and its centers of excellence include applied artificial intelligence, connectivity, Industry 4.0, automation, electronic prototyping and related technology areas. [FIT — Área de Atuação](https://fit-tecnologia.org.br/area-de-atuacao)

The institutional career page states that FIT has a decentralized environment with on-site, remote and hybrid vacancies and explicitly says `Trabalhe de onde estiver`. It also lists home-office support among the benefits. [FIT — Sua Carreira](https://www.fit-tecnologia.org.br/carreiras/sua-carreira)

**Decision:** promote as a primary remote-Brazil R&D career target.

### Radix

Radix states that it uses research and development to create innovations and maintains a dedicated P&D team, research lines, published articles, intellectual-property activity and professionals dedicated to research. [Radix — Inovação](https://radixeng.com.br/a-radix/inovacao)

The company describes `Radix Everywhere` as a remote-first model. Its institutional material says employees are present across Brazil and can work from home, coworking spaces, while travelling or from company offices depending on role and project. [Radix Everywhere](https://radixeng.com.br/a-radix/radix-everywhere)

The recurring careers page links current vacancies and reiterates remote-work support and the Radix Everywhere model. [Radix — Pessoas e carreiras](https://radixeng.com.br/pessoas-e-carreiras)

**Decision:** promote as a primary remote-Brazil R&D career target.

### BairesDev

BairesDev states that it was founded as a remote company, expanded into Brazil in 2011 and now has professionals in more than 50 countries, with Brazil among its largest talent footprints. [BairesDev — About](https://www.bairesdev.com/about/) [BairesDev — Contact](https://www.bairesdev.com/contact-us/)

Its current first-party recruitment portal has recurring fully remote openings. Multiple current listings explicitly describe an internal Research and Development team working on data analysis, machine learning, desktop and web development. Examples include Data Analytics, .NET engineering and Senior Data Scientist roles. [BairesDev — Remote jobs](https://talent.bairesdev.com/) [Senior Data Scientist](https://talent.bairesdev.com/pt/vagas/cientista-de-dados-senior-remote) [.NET / R&D Engineer](https://talent.bairesdev.com/pt/vagas/engenheiro-dotnet-p-d-remote)

The company also states that its global headquarters is in San Francisco while its remote model includes professionals across Latin America and a major teammate footprint in Brazil. [BairesDev — Software outsourcing FAQ](https://www.bairesdev.com/blog/software-outsourcing-top-questions-clients/)

**Decision:** promote as a primary remote-Brazil R&D career target. Its services-company model does not by itself demote it because the verified roles belong to an internal recurring R&D team rather than isolated client assignments.

## Investigated but not promoted

### Fundação CERTI

CERTI is an excellent R&D organization. Its current career page says it works on Research, Technology, Innovation and Entrepreneurship projects, has a research fellowship program tied to PD&I projects and maintains a recurring careers channel. [CERTI — Trabalhe Conosco](https://certi.org.br/trabalhe-conosco/)

However, the same current career page describes its work model as `híbrido e flexível`, while its locations are Florianópolis, Manaus and Brasília. This does not yet establish that a professional can remain based in Curitiba. Historical evidence of full telework is not enough under the radar's current-evidence rule.

**Decision:** keep under investigation until current remote-Brazil eligibility or another Curitiba-compatible arrangement is verified.
