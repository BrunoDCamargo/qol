# R&D Career Radar: sector-agnostic expansion

Date: 2026-09-13

## Scope decision

The Career Radar discovery process is sector-agnostic. Search should not start from a preferred set of industries, technologies or scientific domains. Sector labels are applied after discovery as metadata and do not change priority by themselves.

Curitiba/RMC opportunities and sustained remote-Brazil R&D careers remain equal-priority primary paths. The same evidence gate applies across sectors: meaningful recurring R&D, practical viability while living in Curitiba, and a recurring institutional career path.

This is a project-scoping decision. The durable rule is recorded in `career-radar/FOCUS.md`.

## Promoted in this pass

### Bühler Curitiba

**Verified facts**

- Bühler's official Curitiba location page identifies the site as a production location and application center and explicitly states that local specialists work in R&D, engineering, projects, manufacturing, installation, commissioning and training. The site produces equipment for food processing and grain technologies. [Bühler Curitiba](https://www.buhlergroup.com/global/pt/locations/Brazil-Curitiba.html)
- Bühler maintains R&D as a formal career track and states that more than 1,300 specialists work in its R&D organization globally. [Bühler Research & Development careers](https://www.buhlergroup.com/global/pt/purpose-community/research-and-development.html)
- The company has a recurring institutional jobs portal and current permanent engineering opportunities in Curitiba. [Bühler jobs](https://jobs.buhlergroup.com/) [Current Curitiba mechanical-design role](https://jobs.buhlergroup.com/job-vacancies/desenhista-projetista-mecanico/c0040812-66f1-4670-b02c-7ca7cb92824c)

**Decision**

Promote as a primary Curitiba target. This expands the radar into industrial equipment, food-processing technology, mechanical engineering and process technology without treating those areas as future search filters.

## Search-quality implication

This pass deliberately crossed unrelated domains: food-processing equipment, smart-grid technology, energy services, energy research, health technology, climate/agriculture, chemicals/materials and mathematical optimization. That diversity is useful as a check against search bias, but it is not a quota. Future passes should continue changing vocabulary, sectors and organization types rather than repeatedly querying the categories already dominant in the catalog.
