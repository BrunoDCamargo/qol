# R&D Career Radar: comprehensive sector-agnostic expansion

Date: 2026-09-14

## Scope and method

This pass continues the durable scope defined in `career-radar/FOCUS.md`:

- discovery is sector-agnostic;
- Curitiba/RMC and sustained remote-Brazil work have equal priority;
- promotion requires meaningful recurring R&D/research/development activity, practical viability while living in Curitiba, and a recurring institutional career path;
- sector labels are metadata applied after discovery, not search filters or quotas;
- promotion evidence comes from first-party organization pages or employer-controlled recruiting systems.

The search deliberately crossed health biotechnology, telecommunications hardware, industrial automation/robotics, smart infrastructure, distributed AI systems, speech AI, fraud/identity research, climate/agriculture, fintech and other software/product research environments.

## Promoted: Curitiba and RMC

### Instituto de Biologia Molecular do Paraná (IBMP)

**Verified facts**

- IBMP identifies itself as an Instituição Científica e Tecnológica (ICT) with infrastructure for production and development of health inputs and more than 50 masters and doctors. [O IBMP](https://www.ibmp.org.br/o_ibmp/)
- Its technological-development area explicitly develops in-vitro diagnostics, molecular diagnostics, immunoassays and therapeutic biomolecules. [Desenvolvimento Tecnológico](https://www.ibmp.org.br/desenvolvimento-tecnologico/)
- The institutional careers page lists Desenvolvimento Tecnológico and Inovação em Saúde among its professional areas and links to a recurring vacancies portal. [Oportunidades](https://www.ibmp.org.br/oportunidades/)

**Decision:** promote as a primary Curitiba ICT/biotechnology/health-technology R&D target.

## Search-quality observations

This pass again produced strong candidates in unrelated domains rather than concentrating on whichever sectors were easiest to search. The strict gate remains more important than entry count: companies with research elsewhere but no Curitiba viability, generic remote technical jobs, one-off external recruiter postings, and contractor-only research assignments are not promoted to the primary career layer.
